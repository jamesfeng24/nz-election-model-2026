## D098 — 2026-10-06 — Stage56: manual-adjustment layer and replay guards

The model + James output is produced by a layer on top of the automatic forecast, which stays unmodified and available beside it. An entry is dated, sourced, expiring, append-only and hash-chained; it may move a mean (points of candidate share, on the National/Labour balance or a party share) and may add uncertainty but has no field to remove any: the post-layer coordinate sd is exactly sqrt(automatic^2 + extra^2). A seat with a mean shift must be flagged exceptional and so never inherits the ordinary-seat scale. For the Stage57 replay James labels each historical general seat-election 2014-2023 ordinary or exceptional knowing the outcomes, accepting the selection-bias risk (not relitigated); the guards are a fixed six-fact checklist with a one-line reason per yes and per label, an outcome- and residual-free labelling view, and a hash-pinned freeze before any ordinary-seat scale is estimated. Māori seats are excluded by electorate type. Nothing is estimated and no scale changed. [Interface and tooling](docs/stage56-manual-adjustment-interface.md).

## Manual-adjustment layer (Stage56) — judgement as a separate, dated, additive-uncertainty layer

Human judgement enters only through the dated adjustment layer, never by editing the automatic model or its residual sample. Each adjustment names its target (the National/Labour log-ratio balance or a party's logit share), a mean shift in points of candidate share solved exactly on the draws, and extra uncertainty added in quadrature in that coordinate, so a mean shift alone does not narrow uncertainty (it can still compress a share-space interval near a boundary, which is reported). Adjusted seats are flagged exceptional and excluded from ordinary-seat calibration; both the automatic and the adjusted forecast are retained. The Stage57 replay labels are made with a fixed pre-election checklist, an outcome- and residual-free view, and a hashed freeze before any ordinary-seat scale is estimated; the selection-bias risk of labelling with outcomes known is accepted, recorded, and addressed only by those procedural guards and the 2026 forecast as the real test.
