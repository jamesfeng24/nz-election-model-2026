- Verify's `live` job failed on main after #122 (and on its re-run): `scripts.nowcast_assembly.run --check` reported a stale development gate. Cause: the gate compared the exact `bankDigest`, which hashes every float bit for bit, and runners with AVX2-only CPUs get OpenBLAS results about 1e-14 relative different from the AVX-512 machine that generated it (all 6,851 floats agree to 1.4e-14, no other field differs); PR runs had landed on AVX-512 runners. `--check` now compares a new `bankDigestRounded` (6 decimal places) and the exact digest stays in the file; the check passes under five OpenBLAS kernels (SkylakeX, Haswell, Sandybridge, Nehalem, Zen). `data/processed/nowcast-assembly/development-gate.json` gains that one field; nothing else in it changed. The fixture and readout checks are tolerance-based and were unaffected.
