## D086 — 2026-10-06 — Identity resolution and transition classification for candidate-change evidence

For the incumbent-to-successor evidence pass, same-person identity is decided on evidence and judgement, not exact strings: Stage26 name_match flags accept matches automatically; macron, spelling, nickname, Pacific title and name-order variants are curated with recorded confidence and reasoning; any remaining difference must be a curated change, and a curated change on a same-person row fails the build. By-election winners who recontest are continuations of the effective incumbent, not newcomers. Undocumented departures are `unknown`, never retirement by default. Evidence uses a dated registry separate from `data/sources.json`. No replacement effect, coefficient or model output is produced or changed; this is evidence acquisition for a later separately authorized stage.

## Stage51 candidate-transition evidence

Stage51 candidate-transition evidence classifies, without fitting anything, every National and Labour electorate incumbent-to-successor outcome across the five adjacent pairs 2008→11 to 2020→23. Incumbents are the electorate winners (general and Māori) at the earlier election; successors are the same party's candidates in the dominant Stage25 successor seat. Same-person identity uses Stage26 name-match flags plus curated, evidence-backed judgements for macron, spelling, nickname, title and name-order variants with recorded confidence; any remaining difference must be a typed change, with by-election winners treated as the effective incumbent and undocumented departures recorded as unknown, never retirement by default. The resulting 75 changes (60 election-time exits) are transition facts for a later separately authorized replacement-dataset stage; no replacement effect or R/S change is estimated, and the evidence is tool-rendered with a dated registry. See [candidate-transition evidence](docs/candidate-transition-evidence.md).
