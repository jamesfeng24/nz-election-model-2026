**What was done.** The one question: can Māori electorate polls (three published for 2026) be turned into per-draw candidate shares and winners, with the poll error calibrated on historical Māori electorate polls? Each poll is closed over its named candidates (undecided and "other" allocated proportionally); shares get a log-share Gaussian error with per-candidate `sigma`, one election-wide shift shared by every Māori Party candidate (`tau`), an unadopted-by-rule constant bias, parameter uncertainty by scaled inverse chi-square per draw, and an unnamed remainder drawn from history. Calibration contrast: Māori Party versus Labour log-odds error in 24 of 25 polls. Party-vote figures, the Māori-roll poll and national Te Pāti Māori support are deliberately unused (no double counting); each draw exposes the standardised shared factor for later optional coupling with the national draw.

**Result.** Election means of the contrast: 2014 -0.07, 2017 -0.11, 2020 +0.34, 2023 +0.45. `sigma` 0.231, `tau` 0.253. Bias not adopted (equal-weight +0.15; leave-one-election-out -0.33 nats; same sign in 2 of 4 elections). Diagnostics flagged and not adopted: non-Māori-Party, non-Labour candidates err 4.0 times what `2 sigma^2` allows (mean +0.30 against Labour); polls 21 or more days out have `sigma` 0.305 (9 polls, 5 df). Backtest (leave one election out, 25 polls): mean predicted poll-leader win probability 0.82 against 0.60 observed (15 of 25; 2 of 7 in 2023), Brier 0.208 against 0.240 for a constant 0.6, so the default is overconfident about poll leaders. 2026 default (100,000 draws, seed 2026066): Hauraki-Waikato Maipi-Clarke 0.88; Te Tai Hauāuru Ngarewa-Packer 0.78, Katene 0.22; Te Tai Tonga Ramsden 0.85, Murch 0.11, Te Morenga 0.03, Ferris 0.02. ## D099 — 2026-10-06 — Stage66: Māori seat layer from electorate polls; bias term not adopted

The Māori electorates are modelled completely separately from the general electorates. Each polled seat's electorate poll is closed over its named candidates and turned into per-draw candidate shares with a Gaussian log-share error whose scale was calibrated on 25 historical Māori electorate polls (2014 to 2023) against official results, with one election-wide shift shared by all Māori Party candidates for cross-seat dependence and parameter uncertainty propagated per draw. The design and decision rule were frozen before any error was displayed. A Māori Party-versus-Labour bias term is adopted only if it improves leave-one-election-out scoring by 1.0 nat and has a consistent sign in three of four elections; it did neither (-0.33 nats, 2 of 4), so `b = 0` and the shift is carried as extra shared uncertainty (`tau` computed about zero). Party-vote figures, the Māori-roll poll and national Te Pāti Māori support are not used to move seat shares; the only cross-seat link is the shared shift, and its standardised factor is exported for an optional later coupling. Unpolled seats stay `unpolled` with no values and no fallback.

Recorded caveats: the default model is overconfident about poll leaders on its leave-one-election-out backtest (0.82 predicted against 0.60 observed over 25 polls); minor candidates err far more than the model's noise allows (Te Tai Tonga, the polled four-way seat, is the sensitive one: Labour win 0.63 to 0.91 across arms); the Māori Party candidate outperformed the Labour-relative poll mostly in the two Curia elections, a pollster-era hypothesis that is confounded and not adopted. Nothing is published; probability release, any adoption of an era or bias adjustment and the coupling with the national draw are James's decisions. [Design](docs/stage66-maori-seat-layer-design.md); [findings](docs/stage66-maori-seat-layer-findings.md).

## Stage66 Māori electorate seat layer (D099)

Estimand: for each polled Māori electorate, per draw, each named candidate's share of valid candidate votes and the winner. Conversion: shares closed over the named candidates (undecided and "other" allocated proportionally; the official comparator is closed over the same set; candidates listed at 0% are dropped). Error: `log v_i^c = log q_i + 1[MP](b + u_e) + eps_i` renormalised, `eps ~ N(0, sigma^2)`, `u_e ~ N(0, tau^2)` shared across seats in an election, `b = 0` unless the registered rule adopts the estimate (leave-one-election-out improvement of 1.0 nat and the same sign in three of four elections). Calibrated on 24 Māori Party-versus-Labour contrasts: `sigma^2` from within-election variance (20 df), `tau^2` by method of moments over four elections, both redrawn per Monte Carlo draw by scaled inverse chi-square. Sampling error is not added: the historical errors contain it. The unnamed remainder is drawn from its empirical historical distribution. General-electorate scales and the Stage45 to Stage48 pipeline are not used. Diagnostics reported but never adopted: minor-candidate noise ratio, horizon sensitivity, leave-one-election-out backtest.
