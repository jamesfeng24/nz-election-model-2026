## Stage48 — candidate-balance scale adjustment

The balance seat scale is multiplied by `exp(a + b_R(x_R-m_R) + b_T(x_T-m_T))`, with `x_R` one minus the mean supported R of the National/Labour pair and `x_T` the continuous historical non-major support proxy, both outcome-free. Parameters are fitted on earlier elections only by penalised Gaussian likelihood: each earlier environment enters with the frozen scales in force for it, the shared election effect is integrated (`Sigma_e = diag(sigma_i^2) + sigma_shared^2 11^T`), the conditional logistic location is recomputed for every candidate multiplier so the frozen arithmetic ratio mean is preserved, environments and seats are equally weighted, and ridge sd 0.5 on each log coefficient (bounded ±log 4). Only the balance seat scale changes; shared scale, mass, remainder, means and the national engine are unchanged and the same common random streams are reused. A descriptive likelihood-only constant is reported for context and is never a restriction. Scores are CRPS of the National and Labour candidates, N/L interval scores and coverage, complete-vector energy and the prediction-time margin, on identical records, with a frozen materiality rule (0.01pp major CRPS), coverage and energy guards, fold consistency and a draw-resolution check. Finite-bank numerical gates and the composed precision limitation are reported separately and not relaxed. [Findings](docs/stage48-balance-scale-findings.md).
