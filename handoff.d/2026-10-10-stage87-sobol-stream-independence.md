# Stage87 Sobol stream independence — review-ready, 2026-10-10

Branch `claude/stage87-sobol-stream-independence-i9cw96` from main `50cb5e8`. Requested by James (audit finding); nothing else on main or in open PRs touched the streams.

**Finding.** `GroupedBank` in `scripts/nowcast_assembly/streams.py` built one scrambled Sobol engine per key group (the shared group, plus one per seat and layer), each seeded differently. Scrambling does not make engines independent when paired by row: the leading output bits of every dimension depend only on the low bits of the row index, so engines over the same 65,536 rows agree in coarse structure. Measured at production size on 8-dimension stand-ins: largest row-paired correlation of normal scores between groups 0.98 (shared vs candidate:seat), 0.92 (shared vs party:seat), 0.92 (candidate:seat vs candidate:seat); every group's leading bit agrees with every other group's in 100% of rows (or disagrees in 100%). On a registry shaped like the live one (1,477 keys, 129 groups), 6.7% of cross-column pairs exceeded 4 standard errors, rms correlation 0.17 against 0.0039 for independent columns. Row *r* is also the national draw *r*/16, but the national draws are randomly permuted, so national-versus-layer pairing was never affected. The Māori layer and the national draws do not use these banks; the single-engine Stage46/54/63 evaluation banks are not affected (their historical scramble replicates are not touched here).

**Fix.** Each non-shared group's rows are permuted by `permutation(count, namespace, 'layers-rows:' + group)`. Every column keeps its exact stratification and the dimensions of a group stay jointly low-discrepancy; only row pairing across groups is randomised. Chosen over one joint engine of all 1,477 keys: that needs 0.77 GB at 65,536 rows and still leaves some pairs badly dependent in high dimension (largest |r| 0.12, 0.25 on squares), whereas the permuted design is at noise level for all 1.07 million cross pairs (largest |r| 0.021, rms 0.0039 = 1/sqrt(65,536)). No configuration or schema change.

**Effect (Stage77 rehearsal chain at production size, 4,096 × 16 rows, same national draws and shared noise, before versus after; the rehearsal's candidate lists and classification are SYNTHETIC, so absolute numbers describe no 2026 outcome).**
- Seat win probabilities (197 candidate-seats with a probability above 0.01 in either run): largest single shift 0.137, rms 0.033, mean -0.0004 (no net direction); 64 candidate-seats between 0.1 and 0.9: largest 0.110, rms 0.036. The shifts are far outside the Monte Carlo error (about 0.003), because the noise a seat actually received had the wrong variance (shared and seat noise were correlated per seat).
- Party expected seats and 80% seat intervals: medians identical, intervals identical except Labour's lower end (48 to 47). Bloc P(majority): LAB+GRN 0.452 to 0.473, LAB+GRN+TPM 0.879 to 0.863, NAT+ACT+NZF 0.0025 to 0.0035, NAT+ACT 0 to 0; hung parliament 0.119 to 0.134 (Monte Carlo SE 0.003 to 0.004). Mean overhang 20.2 to 20.4. Bloc 80% intervals move by at most one seat at an end.
- Interval coverage cannot be measured without a backtest and none was run (the historical layer scales were fitted on single-engine banks, so they are not affected by this change).

## D126 — 2026-10-10 — layer-noise groups take independent row orders (Stage87)

Separately seeded scrambled Sobol engines are not independent when paired by row, so the 2026 layer-noise key groups are each read in an independent seeded permutation of the rows of their own Sobol bank (shared group in natural order). Alternative rejected: one joint engine of every key (0.77 GB at production size and still leaves pairs with |r| up to 0.12). Consequence: seat win probabilities in the assembly change (largest shift 0.14 in the synthetic rehearsal), bloc probabilities by at most 0.02. Number provisional, from the coordinator's brief.
