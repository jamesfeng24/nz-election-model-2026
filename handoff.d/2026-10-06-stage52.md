## Stage52 — primary poll capture and verification (acquisition only)

No estimation. Raw publisher, pollster and Wikipedia pages are fetched once with an identifying User-Agent, stored byte-for-byte with response headers and SHA-256, and listed in a deterministic ledger with every failed attempt. `scripts/polling/primary_capture.py` then checks each reported fact (fieldwork dates, sample size, mode, party shares, Māori seat results) as verbatim text in the preserved bytes and fails otherwise; absence is recorded as missing, never inferred or zero-filled. Wikipedia and publisher agreement verifies a poll's metadata but adds no independent measurement, and derivative scrapes add none either. Māori seat polls (Whakaata Māori–Curia, n=500, ±4.5%) are preserved raw only: question, denominator and dependence are for a later authorized stage. No pollster average, house effect or recent-window filter is applied; the recent-polls-only suggestion is recorded as a live-fit sensitivity input (D085).
