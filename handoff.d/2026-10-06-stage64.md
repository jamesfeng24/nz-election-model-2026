## Stage64 — 2026 electorate set and notional 2023 baselines (audit), 2026-10-06

- Audit of what the repo already holds for the 2026 boundaries; data and audit only. The electorate set is correct: 64 general + 7 Māori = 71, equal to the preserved Schedule C by scope, code, name and electoral population (all within ±5% of quota), 49 list seats implied.
- No official notional 2023 results were found (one bounded search; the Electoral Commission site cannot be read directly, so absence is not proved). The repo's own population-weighted reconstruction (Stage4 bounds, Stage41 coherent scenario) is the baseline: every one of the 71 seats has a complete 17-party notional party-vote baseline, and for all 17 parties it reconciles exactly with the official 2023 totals (valid party votes 2,851,211 = 2,659,474 general + 191,737 Māori; source mass, scenario targets and summed bounds all agree).
- Old-to-new mapping documented for all 133 population edges and 72 source seats: 10 renames by plurality predecessor and Ōhāriu abolished, matching the independent third-party mapping for all 71 seats; Kenepuru's plurality predecessor (Mana vs Ōhāriu) is not identified by the bounds.
- Independent cross-check against one preserved third-party notional (Tally Room; comparison only, never a model input): the party-vote top two agree in 70 of 71 seats (Kapiti differs: repo Labour +0.5pp, third party National +7.6pp). Log ratios agree to rounding at the 16 certified-exact seats (max 0.0027) but differ by RMSE 0.106 (max 0.451, Botany) at the 55 other seats, 10 of them large. Cause: within-source heterogeneity that the population-flat reconstruction and its bounds do not represent.
- Boundary codes are vintage-specific (58 general codes denote a different seat in 2020 and 2025); name-keyed joins inventoried, none changed.
- New `scripts/electorate_baseline/`, outputs `data/processed/electorate-baseline/` (register, mapping, reconciliation, audit), standalone registry `data/processed/electorate-baseline/source-registry.json`, raw comparison files `data/raw/electorate-baseline/2026-10-06/`, doc `docs/stage64-electorate-baseline-audit.md`, tests `scripts/tests/test_stage64_electorate_baseline.py` (19). ## D096 — 2026-10-06 — Stage64: 2026 electorate set and notional baseline audited; baseline retained

Recorded: boundary codes are vintage-specific and must not be joined across vintages; 2023 source seats and 2026 targets are joined by election-local and `nz-<scope>-2026-boundary-<code>` ids.
