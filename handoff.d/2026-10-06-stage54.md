## Stage54 — composed Monte Carlo precision

A composed bank is a set of draws each pairing one cached national draw with one point of a scrambled Sobol stream (seed `460046 + year + 1000 s`, unchanged Stage46 key registry) driving the local-party and candidate shocks. Precision is studied with 512-draw blocks: block `b` takes the slice `[512b, 512(b+1))` of the Stage47 order of the balanced 4,096 national subset and scramble `s = b`; layer-only replicates reuse national block 0. MCSE of a quantity is the sample sd across blocks (s512); the nested union of blocks gives the 512/1,024/2,048/4,096 ladder and the frozen doubling caps; the layer share is the variance share of re-scrambles on fixed national draws; the chain split recomputes the 4,096 estimate on each cached chain (reported only). Required draws use `N = smallest power of two with 3 s512 sqrt(512/N) <= cap`. A composed paired difference is settled by the 95% t interval over four blocks against the 0.01pp Stage48 materiality; seat-win probability precision uses the design effect `D = sum 512 s^2 / sum p(1-p)` over probabilities in [0.05, 0.95]. Precision is relative to the cached national draws, not the national posterior. [Findings](docs/stage54-composed-precision-findings.md).
