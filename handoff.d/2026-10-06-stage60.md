**What was done.** The one question: does a stronger global shrink of the candidate-balance seat scale than Stage48's penalised 0.93 to 0.95 improve calibration and proper scores (roadmap plan item 2)? Arms on the Stage48 component harness, scored on all 257 general-electorate candidate records (64/64/65/64) with the 193 seats of 2017/2020/2023 deciding: control 1.00, fixed 0.95/0.90/0.85/0.80, `free` (Stage48 objective with the ridge removed, `a` fitted on earlier elections only, 1.0 in 2014), and Stage48's K as a reference. ## D092 — 2026-10-06 — Stage60: a stronger candidate-balance shrink is recommended, not adopted

Stage60 scored a stronger global shrink of the candidate-balance seat scale than Stage48's penalised constant, under a design frozen before any arm was scored: fixed multipliers 0.95/0.90/0.85/0.80, a penalty-free constant fitted on earlier elections only, and Stage48's K as a reference, on the same candidate-layer harness (neutral R per Stage55, Māori electorates excluded by electorate type). Every candidate arm beats the control on pooled N/L CRPS, interval score and energy and improves all three decision elections. Under the frozen rule (Stage48 IMPROVES plus a per-election coverage floor, 2014 included, and the least aggressive arm within noise of the best) the finding is `recommend_free_for_james_signoff`: the earlier-trained penalty-free constant (multipliers 0.834, 0.854, 0.782; 2026 refit 0.790) lowers pooled N/L CRPS by 0.0616pp (1.80%) and narrows N/L 90% intervals from 23.73 to 21.40pp. The fixed 0.80 arm is blocked by the 2014 80% coverage floor.

Recorded reading, not a rule output: the fixed grid and the leave-future-out fit agree on about 0.78 to 0.85, the shrink is near its limit (2014 and 2020 coverage, pooled 80% coverage 0.878 still above nominal), and the composed National 90% width falls by about 6% to 10% (arithmetic from the Stage47 ablation, not a bank). Nothing is adopted: the corrected control stays the development default and `operationalAdoption` stays null until James signs off. Student-t, mixtures, regimes, sigma shrinkage, geography fragmentation, candidate-quality scores and national MCMC reruns stay closed; the conditional/variance-predictor line stays closed (Stage48). [Findings](docs/stage60-balance-shrink-findings.md); [frozen design](docs/stage60-balance-shrink-design.md).

## Stage60 candidate-balance scale: recommended multiplier (not adopted)

The candidate-balance seat scale of the Stage45 Gaussian law has a recommended earlier-trained global multiplier on the frozen seat scale: the penalty-free constant `exp(a)` of the Stage48 likelihood, trained on all earlier elections (0.834, 0.854, 0.782 for the 2017, 2020 and 2023 targets; 0.790 refit on all four elections for 2026).
