## D133 — 2026-10-11 — an electorate-poll-only refresh is a new forecast, dated by the seat polls (number provisional; the coordinator confirms)

James asked on 2026-10-11 that the weekly chain still update when only electorate polls are new. Decision: the forecast's data cutoff is `seatPolls.pollCutoff`, the later of the adopted national cutoff and the adopted electorate-poll run's date, and every date-dependent seat-poll step (the data-cutoff filter, the age of each poll, the Māori layer's latest poll, the readout, the site evidence) reads it; the national fit and its cutoff (`national.dataCutoff`) are unchanged, because the national state does not depend on the date without new national polls. The release id, the frozen folder and the banner use the same date. A refresh with neither kind of new poll still releases nothing. Alternatives rejected: carrying the national run forward with a new date (it would edit a frozen Stage70 output and imply a refit that did not happen) and leaving electorate-only weeks unpublished (new seat polls would sit unused, and James's weekly step would do nothing visible). Consequence: a forecast can say "National polls to <date>" and "electorate polls to <later date>" together. The 6-week half-life of D117/D123 is unchanged.
