## D084 — 2026-10-06 — Exact MMP allocation under Electoral Act ss 191–193

Stage49 verifies the seat-allocation rules against the Electoral Act 1993 (version 238.0 as at 1 January 2026) and implements them as a rule-only, DOM-free, serializable allocator in `src/models/mmp/allocate.ts`; it makes no forecast and fits nothing. Decisions: a party qualifies with at least 5% (inclusive, integer test) of valid party votes of all listed parties or one constituency win; Sainte-Laguë takes the highest `120 − (independent or off-ballot constituency winners)` quotients, so such winners sit inside the 120 (s 191(8)); overhang follows s 192(5), with other entitlements unchanged and Parliament equal to 120 plus the excess; an exact tie at the cut-off is returned as an explicit `tie-at-cutoff` result because the Act settles it by lot, never resolved by the code; exhausted lists produce unfilled seats (s 193(4)). The Act text is preserved under `data/raw/legislation/` and registered in the standalone `data/processed/mmp/source-registry.json`, not `data/sources.json`. The six official 2008–2023 seat tables reproduce exactly.

Not decided or implemented: component-party mapping, the by-election seat after a cancelled electorate poll, list ordering and eligibility, the 2026 ballot roster, and how uncalibrated candidate-layer winner frequencies feed qualification in the later MMP assembly (candidate-layer named minor-party winner output does not yet exist for 2026). See [mmp-rules-verification.md](docs/mmp-rules-verification.md) and [mmp-allocation-core.md](docs/mmp-allocation-core.md). D074–D082 and all operational nulls are unchanged (D083 is reserved for PR54's Stage47 decision).

## Stage49 — MMP seat-allocation rules and exact allocator

Rule-only component, not a statistical model: no estimand, coefficient, uncertainty or fitted quantity. Inputs are valid party votes per listed party, constituency seats won per party and independent/off-ballot winners; the output is seat counts under Electoral Act 1993 ss 191–193 (qualification at ≥5% or one constituency win, Sainte-Laguë over the highest 120 quotients less independent winners, list seats = entitlement − constituency seats, overhang, list exhaustion). Quotients are compared by exact integer cross-multiplication; exact cut-off ties are flagged, not resolved. Validation: exact replay of the six official 2008–2023 seat tables (four overhang cases) plus hand-worked, boundary and seeded accounting tests. Dependencies: none on other effects; later assembly must supply electorate winners and party votes from the shared simulation draws without double-counting national uncertainty. Not covered: by-election seat after a cancelled poll, component parties, list ordering. See [mmp-rules-verification.md](docs/mmp-rules-verification.md) and [mmp-allocation-core.md](docs/mmp-allocation-core.md).

Statute-verified rules (ss 191–193, 153A–153E, 179): qualification by ≥5% of valid party votes of all listed parties **or** one constituency win (including component-party wins); Sainte-Laguë divisors 1, 3, 5, …; highest 120 quotients, less independent/off-ballot constituency winners (s 191(8)); ties at the cut-off by lot; entitlement minus constituency seats gives list seats; a party whose constituency seats equal or exceed entitlement gets no list seats and keeps them (overhang, Parliament = 120 + excess); exhausted lists leave seats unfilled (s 193(4)). Electoral Commission pages (How are MPs elected?, Sainte-Laguë explained 2017, 2023 official results, boundaries finalised 8 Aug 2025) and the Ministry of Justice summary of the Electoral Amendment Act 2025 corroborate; they were read through a summarising fetch tool and are not preserved. 2026: 64 general + 7 Māori = 71 electorates, 49 nominal list seats (derived).
