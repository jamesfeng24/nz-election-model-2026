## D090 — 2026-10-06 — Stage55: neutral R retained for an ordinary retirement; nothing adopted

Stage55 tested whether the neutral centred R assumption is right for an ordinary same-party National/Labour incumbent replacement, on the Stage44 candidate layer, with a design frozen before any fit or score: 47 general-seat retirements from the Stage51 ledger (scandal-tagged exits, boundary-change successors, by-election successions, party changes and Māori seats excluded by rule), arms fitted on earlier target years only (neutral control C, constant shift K, partial transfer P with `rho` in [0, 1], full transfer T as reference), scored on the N/L balance coordinate of the 31 affected seats in 2017, 2020 and 2023 under the unchanged Stage45 scales. Result: K vs C -0.40% CRPS but in 1 of 3 elections and with an interval spanning zero (MIXED); P is 20% worse than C and T 36% worse (all WORSE; same direction in both sensitivity samples). The frozen rule therefore returns `mixed_report_to_james`.

Recorded reading, not a rule output: neutral R is right on average for a retirement successor (mean `R_new - mu_f` +0.43pp, 90% interval -0.51 to +1.39pp), the descriptive outgoing-to-successor slope (pooled 0.44) is unstable across elections and adding it on top of the layer's existing S transfer degrades scores, and a constant shift is not distinguishable from neutral. Recommendation: keep neutral centred R for ordinary retirement successors; do not add a transfer or shift. Nothing is adopted: `selectedOperationalReplacementEffectPP` stays `null` and Stage10's -6.64pp stays undeployed; the choice between accepting that recommendation and treating K as an open weak signal is James's.

Also recorded: the three Stage51 changes absent from the Stage10 inventory are macron name-key mismatches (Stage10 pairs seats by exact name, 2008 names lack macrons), affecting 8 of 63 general seats in the 2008-11 transition; Stage51 and Stage55 join by occurrence ID and are unaffected, Stage10 outputs are unchanged. Untested observation, not acted on: ordinary-retirement seats show much smaller standardised balance residuals than the control scale assumes (0.29 vs 0.70 across 193 layer seats), which bears on the width track. [Findings](docs/stage55-replacement-effect-findings.md); [frozen design](docs/stage55-replacement-effect-design.md).
