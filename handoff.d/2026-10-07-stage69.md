## Voting-place notional baselines (Stage69)

Each 2023 general seat's voting places are located by geocoding, and each meshblock of that seat (2025 target seat, released random-rounded electoral population) is assigned to its nearest located place. A place's votes go to the new seats in proportion to its catchment population, seat by seat so old-seat totals are conserved exactly. Non-place votes (overseas, specials, unlocated and roving rows, empty-catchment sites) are allocated by the population flows, with the vote-weighted place flows as a sensitivity arm; the 1,000 draws vary place position, that mixture weight and the disclosure intervals. Māori seats are not allocated this way because Māori-roll electors vote at any place. Estimand: the 2023 party vote and candidate vote re-expressed on the 2026 boundaries. Dependencies: the preserved 2023 place tables, Nominatim locations, the Stage4 meshblock frame and the 2020 and 2025 polygons. Uncertainty is carried as draws and arms, never as a point.
