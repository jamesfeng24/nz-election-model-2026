## Stage63 — layer-replicated composed simulation

A replicate is a 4,096-point scrambled Sobol layer stream (Stage46 key registry, seed `460046 + year + 1000 r`) paired position by position with the 4,096 cached national draws in the Stage47 order; arm M is the union of the first M replicates (`4,096 M` composed draws, national-only control mean as the point forecast). The local-party layer's conditional-location solves depend only on the national draw, so within a task they are computed once and reused exactly across replicates (keyed by the full input bytes, tags and scales; bit-identical to the uncached path); the candidate layer's solves are never shared. Layer precision is the change when M/2 is doubled to M at the fixed pool (gate B), with the 3-sigma requirement `smallest power of two M with 3 s1 / sqrt(M) <= cap` from the sd `s1` across single-replicate banks; the finite national bank leaves a floor estimated from eight 512-draw national blocks pooled over 64 replicates as `max(0, var_b(T_b) - mean_b(var_r(T_b(1))) / 64) / 8`; win-probability SEs combine a layer design effect and a national design effect (`SE = sqrt(0.25 (Dn + D1/M) / 4096)`). [Findings](docs/stage63-layer-replication-findings.md).
