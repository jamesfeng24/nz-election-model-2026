## Weekly poll refresh (Stage70)

The national nowcast input is refreshed as polls appear: the pinned Wikipedia table is captured, new rows are matched to the previous panel by wave id, the unchanged Stage62 pipeline (pinned upstream parse, gauss model, Stage38 settings and gates) is rerun with cutoff equal to the run date, and the latent national state at the latest poll week (`lastDataSupport`, which integrates house-effect and industry polling-error uncertainty) is saved with its model-state date. Election-week draws, any extra polling-error draw and campaign drift are excluded. Intervals and estimates are poll-model output, uncalibrated at this horizon; each refresh also reports its change against the previous published estimate against the roughly 0.1 pp Monte Carlo noise floor. See `docs/stage70-weekly-poll-refresh.md`.
