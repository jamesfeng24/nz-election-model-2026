# George Hawkins' 2011 Election Announcement

George Hawkins announced his retirement from Parliament on **September 29, 2011**, when he delivered his valedictory statement to the House. Rather than formally announcing a non-contest decision earlier, Hawkins first won election to the Manurewa Local Board in 2010 and stated he would resign from Parliament if successful. He then "proactively ended his ministerial career" after the 2005 election by refusing reappointment as minister, but continued as an MP. His retirement came after he secured Louisa Wall's selection as his successor with his blessing.
