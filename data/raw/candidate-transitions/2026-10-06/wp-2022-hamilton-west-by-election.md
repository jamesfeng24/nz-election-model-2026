# 2022 Hamilton West By-Election Summary

**Why the seat became vacant:**
Former Labour MP Gaurav Sharma resigned on 18 October 2022. He had been "expulsion from the party caucus" after breaching confidentiality of caucus communications in August 2022.

**Winner:**
Tama Potaka from the National Party won with 6,974 votes (46.29%).

**Previous holder:**
Labour held the seat before this election. Gaurav Sharma, the departing MP, had represented Hamilton West since 2020.

**By-election date:**
The by-election was held on 10 December 2022, as announced by Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern on 20 October 2022.

**Election significance:**
Media and opposition parties interpreted Potaka's victory as indicating "waning support for Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern's administration," though the result was marked by notably low turnout at 31.4%.
