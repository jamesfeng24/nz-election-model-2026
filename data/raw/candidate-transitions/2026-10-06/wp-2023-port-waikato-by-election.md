# 2023 Port Waikato By-Election: Key Facts

**Why voting was postponed:**
"Christensen's death was announced on 9 October 2023, one week into the advanced voting period." This triggered the delay, as the candidate death occurred after nomination closure but before the general election polling day.

**National candidate:**

**Election winner:**
Andrew Bayly won decisively. "Final results show Andrew Bayly of the National party winning with a 11,432 vote margin over his nearest rival, Casey Costello of New Zealand First." He secured 76.33% of the vote on "25 November (Saturday)" when the by-election was held.
