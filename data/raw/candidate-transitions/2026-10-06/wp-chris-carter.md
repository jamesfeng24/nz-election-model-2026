# Chris Carter's Departure from Labour and 2011 Election

Carter was suspended from the Labour caucus on 29 July 2010 for allegedly authoring an anonymous letter claiming a leadership challenge against Phil Goff.[^1] He was officially declared an independent MP on 17 August 2010,[^2] then expelled from the Labour Party on 11 October 2010.[^3] Rather than standing in the 2011 election, Carter resigned from Parliament on 30 September 2011 following his appointment to a United Nations position in Afghanistan, making him ineligible to contest the election.[^4]
