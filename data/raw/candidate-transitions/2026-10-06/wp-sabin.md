# Mike Sabin's 2015 Resignation from Parliament

Mike Sabin stepped down from his Northland seat on January 30, 2015, stating he was resigning "due to personal issues that were best dealt with outside Parliament." (Scoop, "Mike Sabin announces resignation as Northland MP," January 30, 2015)

This departure followed media reports in December 2014 regarding a police investigation into an assault complaint, though neither police nor Prime Minister John Key confirmed details publicly.

His resignation triggered a by-election in the Northland electorate, which was subsequently won by Winston Peters, as indicated by the succession table showing Peters followed Sabin in that parliamentary seat.
