# Wall Wins Labour Selection for Manurewa

**Publication Date:** Sunday, December 12, 2010 (Updated Wednesday, July 15, 2026)

**Summary:**

Policy advisor Louisa Wall has secured the Labour Party's nomination for the Manurewa electorate. "Manurewa is held by former minister George Hawkins, who is retiring at the next election."

The selection process was intensive, with Wall emerging victorious after an eight-hour meeting where she competed against six other candidates, including sitting list MP Ashraf Choudhary and local lawyer Ian Dunwoodie.
