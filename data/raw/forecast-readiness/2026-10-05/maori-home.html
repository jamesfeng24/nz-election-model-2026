Nau mai haere mai e te iwi! 

Te Pāti Māori will look after you the way a marae looks after all people. Everyone will have a home, be cared for, protected, connected and empowered. We will be sovereign, and together, we will be the kaitiaki our whenua needs us to be.

Our seven pou set out how we will deliver that vision:

KĀINGA | HOUSED:

More public and affordable homes, support to build on Māori whenua, higher incomes and relief from the rising cost of living.

TAUMARU | CARED FOR:

Māori-led, whānau-centred health and disability services that are accessible, culturally safe and accountable.

TIAKI | PROTECTED:

Justice transformation, stronger protection for mokopuna Māori and an end to laws that criminalise poverty and survival.

HONONGA | CONNECTED:

Investment in te reo Māori, Toi Māori, sport, culture and community.

WHAKAMANA | EMPOWERED:

A fairer tax system, stronger Māori enterprise and better access to education, training and opportunity.

MANA MOTUHAKE | SOVEREIGN:

Te Tiriti-based constitutional transformation and the restoration of Māori authority and decision-making.

KAITIAKI | GUARDIANS OF THE WHENUA:

Protection of our whenua, wai and moana, including banning seabed mining and upholding Māori authority over natural resources.

THIS ELECTION, WE ARE ASKING FOR YOUR CANDIDATE VOTE

A candidate vote for Te Pāti Māori gives us the best chance of delivering three things for Māori:

1. MORE MĀORI IN PARLIAMENT

A candidate vote for Te Pāti Māori gives us the best chance of winning the Māori electorates, while Māori candidates from other parties who are high on their party lists may still enter Parliament too.

That means more Māori voices around the decision-making table.

2. A ONE-TERM GOVERNMENT

Winning the Māori electorates gives us the strongest chance of stopping the current Government from securing another term.

3. AN INDEPENDENT MĀORI VOICE

More Te Pāti Māori MPs will ensure Māori have a strong and independent voice influencing the direction of the next Government.

MOKOPUNA DECISIONS

This election, we are putting our money where our mouth is.

We are putting the future of our mokopuna before political ego.

Your candidate vote gives us the best chance of getting more of our people into Parliament to defend our rights, advocate for our whānau and shape the decisions that affect generations to come.

Te Tiriti Entrenchment

Te Tiriti Entrenchment - Click to read more
What we’ll do Establish a Matike Mai Fund Implement the Untied Nations Declaration on Rights of Indigenous Peoples Establish an Independent Te Tiriti Commission Make Waitangi Tribunal recommendations binding Why it matters Te Tiriti should not depend on who wins an election. For too long, governments have been able to weaken, remove, or ignore their Te Tiriti obligations. Our policy changes that by making the Crown legally accountable while resourcing Māori to determine the constitutional future of Aotearoa for ourselves. This is not about Parliament giving Te Tiriti its authority. Parliament did not create Te Tiriti; it is about binding the Crown to the promises it already made and ensuring that no future government can simply choose to walk away from them. Kai Sovereignty

Kai Sovereignty - Click to read more
What we’ll do Protect Māori food systems (including seed sovereignty, rejecting GE foods, and upholding WAI262). Invest in community food systems and regenerative Māori agriculture. Why it matters Aotearoa grows enough kai to feed everyone, yet too many whānau are goinghungry. That is not scarcity, it is system failure. Kai sovereignty means affordable,healthy kai connected to whenua, whakapapa, and Māori control. To read the full policy Click here

Disabilities - Click to read more

Why it matters Disabled Māori deserve support that honours their mana, their whānau, and their right to live fully. Housing

Housing - Click to read more
What we’ll do Build and support more public and affordable homes. Make it easier to build homes on Māori/ancestral land. Stop speculation and land-banking (including action on vacant properties). Why it matters A warm, safe, secure home is not a luxury. It is the ground everything else stands on: health, education, safety, stability, and hope. Our people deserve homes, not waiting lists, overcrowding, or speculation.

Education & Training

Education & Training - Click to read more
What we’ll do Build Māori-led education authority and solutions. Embed mātauranga Māori properly across learning. Remove barriers that block whānau from education and training. Support Māori governance and decision-making in education. Why it matters Education should never teach our tamariki to leave themselves at the door. It should strengthen identity, grow confidence, open pathways, and prepare our people to lead in the world as Māori.

Toi Māori - Click to read more
