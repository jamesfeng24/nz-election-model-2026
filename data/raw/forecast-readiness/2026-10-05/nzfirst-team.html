Our Team

Following the 2023 general election, New Zealand First secured eight seats in parliament. This is the team that gives a voice to Kiwis who want to be represented by certainty, experience and common sense in parliament. As we head into Election 2026 we have a team of candidates who will spread New Zealand First's values and messages.
