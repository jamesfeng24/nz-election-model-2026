Why New Zealand First?

This election, you have a chance to show the real power of MMP. The two old parties have been around for decades, with Pepsi-and-Coke pendulum-swing majorities

New Zealand First has never had the numbers needed to truly create real change. This election, New Zealanders have a chance to change that.

You can move away from what our system used to be and make a real change to how your country is governed, and who represents you in government.

The question for you is: what do you want our country to be? We can no longer continue to accept an establishment status quo that wants nothing more than to protect the power it thinks it has and maintain the separation between ordinary Kiwis and the elite.

We will never give up that fight against the elitist status quo.
