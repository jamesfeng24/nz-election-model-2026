# Stage46 pre-fit residual-tail diagnosis

2026-10-05. This audit precedes new uncertainty estimation/scoring. Saved Stage45 per-election effects are subtracted **descriptively**, never imported into a held-out forecast. Every valid seat/coordinate remains. Equal election weight, equal seats within elections, remainder coordinates divided by their effective degrees of freedom. Empirical inverse-CDF quantiles and fractional boundary weighting for tail fractions are frozen in `diagnostic-contract.json`; no score-selected bins.

Candidate National/Labour log balance is the clearest proposed correction: seat RMS / Gaussian-equivalent median absolute deviation (MAD) by election is 2014 .289/.235, 2017 .288/.201, 2020 .197/.142, 2023 .248/.190. Equal-election pooled RMS .2583 versus central .2002; the largest 5% contributes 51.29% of squared seat error. Beyond three within-election RMS: 1.953% versus a Gaussian .270% reference. These are descriptive shape checks, not independent calibration replications. Shared movements explain substantial raw error in 2014 and 2020; they remain separate Gaussian effects.

Local-party balance is heterogeneous across elections (2023 central dispersion exceeds RMS), local mass has little pooled centre/tail separation, and candidate mass evidence is inconsistent across elections. Remainder tails shrink substantially after descriptive group standardization. Different minor-party ordinary scales account for much of their pooled shape. No change to those seat directions is justified by this bounded audit. Remainder heterogeneity remains a limitation of the pooled family, not a numerical bug.

The supported experiment is **candidate seat balance only**: one strongly pooled robust central estimator, Student-t with assumed nu=4, and a matched robust-scale Gaussian. Four reused candidate environments cannot reliably identify tail thickness; nu is an explicit finite-variance assumption, not an estimated or searched optimum. No particular seat is deemed exceptional at prediction time. Tāmaki, Epsom, Ōhāriu and every other case remain in training/evaluation. Influential labels describe results only.

Independent audit agrees on the diagnosis. Its preliminary interpolated quantiles differ slightly from the final frozen inverse-CDF convention; this is recorded estimator choice, not tolerance relaxation. No mean model, source, identity or historical output changes.
