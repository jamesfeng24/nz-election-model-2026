# Stage 20 final pre-fit contract: complete-share S/V experiment

**Frozen proposal for separate authorization; no fit or score has been run.** The [applicability inventory](stage20-applicability-findings.md) and its source/feature/rank artifacts were committed before this contract. The [machine contract](../data/processed/checkpoints/complete-share-feature-applicability/fit-contract.json) pins exact training and common evaluation contest **and candidate IDs** plus every rule below. It does not turn Stage 11's matched-component result into a complete-share forecast. Existing operational selections remain null.

## Population, information and feature meaning

The target is each standing candidate's share of **valid candidate votes** in a held general contest on one of the 2008→2011, 2014→2017 or 2020→2023 certified unchanged-boundary transitions. The full frame remains 213 contests: 191 held general, 21 Māori coverage-only and one cancelled. Stage 18's election-local party mapping admits 63/64/44 held general contests in the three target years; its 20 ambiguous 2023 held contests still abstain. The full slate enters any admitted contest, including no-party-group and unsupported entrants. Within each admitted slate all four future restrictions use the **same IDs** and share a single normalization denominator. No source/target candidate-person link is needed.

Target local valid-party shares and the full target slate are supplied **retrospective conditional** inputs, not pre-election predictions. `S0` is the earlier party-ballot row's printed same-party-candidate-destination percentage divided by 100, supported only for a positive exact source row and unique source candidate of a party with an eligible cross-election continuity record. `V0=C0−P0` is the earlier party-seat candidate share minus earlier party share, each from its own valid-vote denominator. It is not candidate competitiveness, identified personal strength or proof that an outgoing candidate transfers support. Party/category entry and affirmative no-party-group cases, or a source party without a candidate destination, receive **neutral feature fallback**. A zero-mass source row makes S unavailable but need not erase independently supported V. Missing source table/eligible row, ambiguous continuity/destination, incomplete target party vector/slate and inherited Stage 18 mapping ambiguity abstain the whole contest. An unavailable feature never becomes a numeric zero observation; its *adjustment term* is omitted for that candidate with fallback marked. Winner flags, target candidate totals/residuals, identities and later evidence cannot alter eligibility or features.

## Four jointly fitted restrictions and centering

For mapped candidate `c`, define `b_c=P1,party(c)+κ`; for an affirmative no-party-group candidate `b_c=κ`. On a given training fold, let `μS` and `μV` be supported-feature means using weight `1/slate_size` per candidate, divided by the total weight of supported candidates, across **earlier training contests only**. When feature S or V is unsupported for `c`, set its *adjustment covariate* to zero; otherwise set `xS_c=S0_c−μS` and `xV_c=V0_c−μV`. Set

`w_c=b_c × exp(θS xS_c + θV xV_c)`, and `m_c=w_c/Σ_d w_d`.

The restrictions are baseline `(κ)`, baseline+S `(κ,θS)`, baseline+V `(κ,θV)` and baseline+S+V `(κ,θS,θV)`. Omitted θ is exactly zero **as a model restriction**, not a claim that the historical effect is zero. Every included parameter is fitted jointly from scratch on the same eligible earlier outcomes; **Stage 18's fitted κ and Stage 8–10 coefficients are never imported**. Compare S and V individually against the refitted baseline, then combined against baseline and against each reduced restriction. Failure of an individual term is not an automatic veto of the predeclared joint test. No interaction, party-specific slope or person coefficient is authorized by this contract.

The 2017 holdout would train on **all 63** admitted 2011 target contests/423 candidates and evaluate 64/431. The 2023 holdout would train on all admitted 2011+2017 target contests (**127/854**) and evaluate 44/312. The earliest 2011 transition has no earlier eligible feature transition, so fitted methods abstain there; uniform is a full-slate parameter-free benchmark and restricted zero-floor applies only to its existing positive-mapped/no-group-free subset. Four-model evaluation samples are identical within each trained fold; missing S/V invokes the declared fallback and is reported, never selectively dropped. Māori, cancelled and ambiguous contest exclusions remain in full-frame coverage. Earlier completed candidate outcomes are permitted only for training later folds; each target holdout's candidate outcomes are evaluation-only.

## Rounded source input and sensitivity

The source split table publishes rounded percentages, not exact joint counts. The **printed decimal** is a declared working approximation for the primary conditional fit, not an arbitrary midpoint or an assertion of exactness. For each positive source party row, every cell has its own clipped `printed ±0.005 percentage-point` envelope and the row must sum to 100%. The selected same-party cell's feasible lower and upper are tightened by all other cells. A deterministic witness for either selected endpoint fixes that cell, initializes the other cells at their lower bounds and fills remaining mass in published column order up to each upper bound. Each witness is a complete feasible source row. Different source party rows can be combined because they are separate reported ballot populations; no independently incompatible endpoints within a row are combined.

The only predeclared measurement sensitivities are **all-row selected lower** and **all-row selected upper** coherent witness scenarios. In each scenario, recompute S, the earlier-training-only centering mean and all four joint fits on the **same IDs**, then evaluate the same holdout. These are source-rounding perturbations, not calibrated intervals for future behaviour. V and all party inputs remain fixed. A printed `0.00` on a positive row is a rounded observed value with its feasible range, not missingness; a zero-mass row remains unsupported. No interval endpoint is selected after inspecting results.

## Deterministic future fitting and failure handling

Use equal-contest mean cross entropy `mean_e[−Σ_c observed_share_ec log(m_ec)]`, with every standing candidate in the normalized slate. Positive κ makes all predictions positive, including when an earlier outcome share is zero. Keep `κ∈[0.0001,0.1]` in party-share units and `θS,θV∈[−4,4]` in log-intensity units per unit fractional feature. The finite θ box is a pre-result guard against nonexistent/extreme unregularized solutions, not a tuned political prior: one unit of S or V can multiply relative intensity by at most `exp(4)≈54.6` per term. Report any boundary optimum and **do not expand** a bound after scoring.

At fixed κ, the softmax cross-entropy objective is convex in the included θ because it is a sum of log-sum-exp terms plus a linear term. Solve that box-constrained subproblem with analytic gradient and deterministic L-BFGS-B from fixed starts `−2, 0, +2` for each included coordinate. Require agreeing objectives within `1e−8` and projected gradient infinity norm at most `1e−7`; a boundary solution uses its projected gradient. Profile over κ with SciPy SHGO, one dimension, Sobol 256 points, two iterations and `f_tol=1e−12`, explicitly evaluating both bounds and every returned local minimum. Independently check a fixed 4,097-point κ grid plus bounded refinement around all sampled local minima. Require objective agreement within `1e−8` and κ agreement within `1e−4`, except a documented flat/tied optimum. Objective ties within `1e−12` choose smaller κ, then lexicographically smaller θ. Nonfinite, rank-deficient or unconverged fits and disagreeing optimizers are **fit abstentions**, not substantive null results or reasons to change the specification. The box, starts, tolerances and gates are frozen before any outcome calculation.

The input-only rank audit found S/V within-slate rank 2 and combined floor/S/V rank 3 on both training folds at κ probes 0.0001, 0.01 and 0.1, with normalized-column condition ratios at most 2.80. Future fitting must recheck rank on the actual common sample, preserve training-only means, and stop if a required column loses rank or condition exceeds `10^6`. This does not ensure stable estimated effects from three election clusters.

## Common evaluation and stopping

All four restrictions use exactly the pinned candidate/contest IDs. Primary MAE is the mean over contests of `100 × mean_c|m_c−s_c|`; primary RMSE is the square root **after** averaging contest `10,000 × mean_c(m_c−s_c)^2`. Report candidate-equal sensitivity, party/category and feature-support signed bias, slate-size strata, full-frame coverage and fallback fractions. Full-slate signed bias cancels and is only an accounting check. Predicted winners are the argmax set at absolute share tie tolerance `1e−12`; report unique-winner accuracy, ties and tied-set inclusion separately. Uniform and the frozen restricted zero-floor comparator remain contextual, on their own transparently reported supported subsets. No probability score or calibrated winner uncertainty is implied.

The development screen for the combined model versus its **jointly refitted baseline on the same holdout IDs** is at least **0.25 pp contest-equal MAE gain in both 2017 and 2023**, no RMSE regression of 0.25 pp or more in either, no loss of applicable coverage, and no practical-gain sign reversal under either coherent rounding scenario. Show the five largest contest-level paired changes per fold and the range of leave-one-contest-out gains; a sign reversal from removing one contest marks the gain influence-dependent, without removing that contest from the primary score. Report each reduced-model ablation on the same sample; do not search subsets or retune floor/link/feature categories. This is a practical development screen, **not** a significance test or operational-selection rule. Only three already inspected election transitions exist; source rows/party categories and seats are dependent. The operational candidate baseline, split view and candidate effects remain null/unselected irrespective of this conditional experiment.
