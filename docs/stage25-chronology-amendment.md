# Stage25 chronology amendment and practical linkage proposal

This is a **post-checkpoint design amendment**, dated 2026-10-03, to PR #32 at `3e63f2472120dc49cef9313cfca2f26a6bf14064`. It changes permitted future training IDs and the next research task. It does not fit, predict, score, acquire evidence, link people or rewrite any Stage16/22 result. The original design remains in Git history; `original-design-contract.json` pins its 30 training/evaluation ID sets and unchanged geography, availability, source and composition bytes. All operational selections remain null or unresolved.

## Why the inherited cutoff exists

Stage16's `specification.json:chronology` and `conditional_nat_lab_response/model.py:training_rows` explicitly require training transition target year **strictly less than the holdout source year**. Stage25 copied this conservative separation. Stage20/22 specify 2017 training on 2011 targets and 2023 training on 2011+2017 targets. With only 2008→2011, 2014→2017 and 2020→2023 available, expanding-window and more-separated protocols yield the same samples. Their historical results therefore do not test the difference.

There is no recorded dependency finding requiring the extra election gap. Nor is there a recorded claim that overlapping transitions inherently leak. The defensible interpretation is an inherited conservative separation design, not a proven information constraint. It omits the latest completed transition when that transition's outcome election becomes the next holdout's source predictor election. This reduces direct election reuse across training and predictors; it does not eliminate repeated seats, party references or all dependence. Do not rewrite this explanation as the original authors' demonstrated intent.

## Two registered protocols

**Primary: `expanding_window`.** Admit an otherwise eligible transition only if its completed target year ≤ holdout source year and < holdout target year. In the five adjacent transitions this admits the immediately preceding completed transition. The detailed audit above supports this for the conditional identity-free questions. No target/later training outcome is permitted. Later implementation must enforce these exact IDs on both fitting and all fitted preprocessing.

**Sensitivity: `more_separated`.** Preserve training target year < holdout source year, with original Stage25 ID sets hash-checked. This is an intentional separation sensitivity, not the only leakage-safe protocol. A saved fixed fit retains its original training contract regardless of which new protocol is primary; a 2017 saved fit transported to 2020 is distinct from either refitted design.

## Next separately authorized implementation: practical candidate linkage

The next stage should build **one supplemental, reversible record-linkage layer from preserved evidence**, before identity-dependent refitting. It does not need new biographies or source acquisition. Freeze the rules and explicit alias table before inspecting model outcomes. Generate the deterministic occurrence-pair universe from preserved candidature and Stage25 certified exact predecessor IDs across all five adjacent transitions, retaining general/Māori and cancelled/missing status separately. Retain every source and target occurrence in the review inventory, including nonmatches; do not limit the frame to inherited confirmed/probable people or winning candidates. Where a same-person seat/party-change lead exists in preserved evidence, keep it as an explicit exception rather than forcing a same-seat/party automatic link.

Proposed automatic acceptance requires all of:

1. Original labels retained; deterministic Unicode NFC, case, punctuation and whitespace normalization. Preserve accents; unfamiliar spelling changes/possible typos remain exceptions, not nickname equivalences.
2. Matching normalized surname and matching noninitial given name, or a frozen recognized nickname equivalence. Missing middle names or compatible middle-name initials may be accepted; conflicting expanded middle names are not compatible. A given-name initial alone is insufficient.
3. Consistent election-local affiliation and independently supported party continuity. Electorate continuity is additional support; it cannot alone identify a person. Shared ballot-group membership alone does not resolve constituent identity. A party switch needs preserved documentary relationship evidence or exception adjudication, not an automatic override.
4. No competing match in either election, homonym collision or contradictory preserved relation/career evidence. Common surnames alone do not trigger research if the complete rule gives a unique compatible match. Actual alternatives/conflicts quarantine the edges for review.

The proposed finite alias table is **Bill↔William, Bob↔Robert, Jim↔James, Mike↔Michael, Pete↔Peter, Steve↔Stephen, Tom↔Thomas, Tony↔Anthony, Chris↔Christopher, Liz↔Elizabeth, Sue↔Susan, Dave↔David**. Treat each pair as an exact equivalence rule requiring the other safeguards, not fuzzy spelling or an automatically extended nickname graph. Freeze this list in the authorized implementation's pre-link contract; any later addition needs a transparent rule amendment before rerunning links, not an outcome-driven exception. More ambiguous shortenings outside the list remain unresolved.

Emit one review table for **every proposed edge**, with original occurrence IDs/names, normalized tokens, party/geographic evidence, exact acceptance rule, competing edges, conflicts, fact/publication/retrieval timing and reversibility. Separate labels:

- `documentary_same_person`: an occurrence-specific relationship bridge supported by a cited preserved passage;
- `accepted_algorithmic_same_person`: deterministic rule accepted, with `exact_name`, `middle_omission`, `middle_initial` or `frozen_nickname` subrule, not relabelled documentary confirmation;
- `unresolved_ambiguous`: insufficient/competing/contradictory evidence, including failure to match;
- `documentary_distinct_people`: independently supported distinct-person evidence, never name difference alone.

Do not automatically union links transitively into a person. Test each proposed edge against all competing/conflicting evidence before assigning any provisional person group; conflicting components remain unmerged. An existing documentary person ID may be referenced without rewriting its historical adjudication. A retrospective algorithmic decision does not establish that identity evidence was published by a historical cutoff. Identity is not complete tenure, first-ever service or proof of replacement.

**Bounded scope and stop:** audit all rule-generated proposals automatically; inspect at most **60 exception cases** in stable order `(transition source year, target year, ambiguity type, source occurrence ID, target occurrence ID)`, with at most one preserved-evidence inspection per exception. No external searches, biography acquisition, replacement cases or easier-case top-ups. Remaining exceptions stay `not_reviewed_budget` or `reviewed_unresolved`. Report that this is a review cap, not a target to spend. Do not claim an unmeasured precision percentage.

Expected new artifacts are `scripts/evidence/practical_candidate_linkage/` (small normalization, proposal and review modules), `data/processed/evidence/practical-candidate-linkage/{contract,occurrences,proposed-links,review,coverage,manifest}.json`, a findings document and focused tests. Pin only consumed preserved records/raw bytes; keep older person links, Stage13/21 ledgers and model outputs unchanged. No fitting adapter is part of that implementation.

Acceptance criteria: all occurrences/proposals reconcile; exact deterministic rule and evidence route per accepted edge; no automatic link with a known conflict/competing match; no outcomes/errors/identity confidence in priority or acceptance; nickname and middle-name fixtures; original-name/raw/adjudication preservation; no transitive conflict bypass; reproducible review ledger and coverage by transition/scope/party/outcome **after** acceptance. The broader accepted algorithmic sample may support later research, with a stricter sensitivity limited to documentary bridges and exact fully matching given/surname tokens, excluding nickname and middle-name concessions. Report coverage gains and actual ambiguities, not invented precision or empirical effect. Unknown career histories remain explicit gates for freshman/replacement-tenure questions.
