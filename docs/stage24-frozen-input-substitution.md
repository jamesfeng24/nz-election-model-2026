# Stage 24 frozen input-substitution contract

This specification and the [machine inventory](../data/processed/checkpoints/stage24-party-input-substitution/input-inventory.json) are committed **before new candidate predictions or scores**. Stage22's saved parameters, training-only means, source S features, candidate slates and election-local ballot-group mapping remain fixed. Stage23's complete party vectors replace **only** target local party support. This is a developmental, conditional input-substitution diagnostic; it does not fit a coefficient or select an operational model.

## Exact population and four cells

Use Stage22's exact 64 common held-general contests and 431 candidate occurrences for target 2017, and 64/459 for target 2023. Every cell and rounding scenario uses these identical IDs. Stage22 has no earlier eligible feature-transition training for a fitted 2011 model, so 2011 has no Stage24 fitted comparison. The full 213-frame is retained for coverage: 191 held general, 21 Māori coverage-only and cancelled Port Waikato. A missing group, duplicate destination, ambiguous mapping, incomplete vector or inconsistent slate abstains the **whole contest in all four cells**; an error is reported, not silently trimmed.

For candidate `c` in contest `e`, let `p_obs[e,g]` be the Stage22 observed target local valid-party share for its **ballot group** `g`, and `p_pred[e,g]` the Stage23 constructed local party share matched through its explicit canonical-category-to-election-local-ballot-group key. An affirmative no-party-group candidate has party input zero under both. This zero is a ballot-category fact, not an inference of zero candidate strength. Retain each Stage22 scenario's saved `κ_baseline`, `κ_S`, `θS`, training-only `μS` and candidate's printed or coupled-witness `S0`; unsupported S uses adjustment zero. For input `p`, the baseline intensity is `p[e,g(c)] + κ_baseline`; S-only intensity is `(p[e,g(c)] + κ_S) × exp(θS(S0_c−μS))` when S is supported and the baseline factor alone otherwise. Divide each intensity by the sum across the **complete standing slate**. Candidate affiliation is not a ballot-group key. An officially shared group with one local constituent destination is used once. There is no additional normalization of party inputs, new fallback, fit or candidate effect.

The cells are **A** baseline/observed local party, **B** S-only/observed, **C** baseline/Stage23 local party, and **D** S-only/Stage23 local party. Reproduce A and B against the saved Stage22 predictions at absolute share tolerance `1e-12` for the printed and each selected-lower/selected-upper scenario **before** constructing C/D. Disagreement stops the run. The two endpoint scenarios retain their own saved fits and training means and are coherent source-rounding witnesses, not predictive intervals.

## Metrics, comparisons and fixed descriptions

For each contest use `100 × mean_candidate |predicted−actual|` as contest MAE and `10000 × mean_candidate (predicted−actual)²` as contest MSE. Holdout MAE is the equal-contest mean; RMSE is the square root of equal-contest mean MSE. Candidate-equal MAE and RMSE are reported separately. Report all four cells, paired B−A, D−C, C−A and D−B MAE and MSE differences, and RMSE differences of aggregate scores. Negative paired MAE means improvement. The descriptive difference-in-differences is `I = (MAE_D−MAE_C)−(MAE_B−MAE_A)`, independently checked as the equal-contest mean of `(D_i−C_i)−(B_i−A_i)`. Positive I weakens S's relative MAE advantage under substituted inputs; negative strengthens it. Pooled scores weight all 128 contests equally (64 per fold); pooled candidate-equal sensitivity weights 890 candidates equally. No formal independence, causality or new success threshold is inferred.

Fixed groups are National (`nationalparty`), Labour (`labourparty`), their combined set, other mapped party/group candidates, and affirmative no-party-group candidates. Group errors are candidate-equal by default; a contest-equal sensitivity averages only contests containing that group and reports its contest denominator. For each group, report counts, four-cell MAE/RMSE/signed bias, C−A and D−B substitution damage, B−A and D−C relative S error and I. Group scores need not sum to whole-slate scores. A no-group candidate's share can move through the common slate denominator even though its party input remains zero.

For NAT/LAB only, inspect the paired change in **absolute candidate error** against `100×(p_pred−p_obs)` and retain candidate-level diagnostic records. Fixed party-error bins are `≤−5`, `(−5,−2]`, `(−2,0]`, `(0,2]`, `(2,5]`, `>5` percentage points. Report counts and mean paired candidate-error changes for each bin and party. Actual local party support is evaluation-only for this input-error diagnostic; it cannot select cases or modify C/D. Do not fit a smoother or move bins after seeing results.

Use Stage22's absolute share tie tolerance `1e-12`. For each cell report predicted tie count, tied-set inclusion of the actual winner, unique-predicted-winner count and unique-correct count divided by all contests. For A→C and B→D report changed predicted-winner sets and correct→incorrect/incorrect→correct transitions (with ties explicit). The complete-slate margin diagnostic is absolute error, in pp, of the **actual winner's predicted share minus the strongest predicted other candidate** against the actual winner's observed share minus the strongest observed other. This can be negative on the prediction side. Report mean absolute margin error over all contests and five largest errors; do not use actual winner in prediction construction.

Show the five largest absolute contest-level I values and substitution damage values. Report I's min, fixed quantiles 25/50/75 and max using linear interpolation, plus the leave-one-contest-out range of I and of the B−A/D−C paired MAE differences. Keep every contest in primary scores. Repeat the same tables for printed and two saved rounding scenarios; do not choose a scenario. Carry forward Stage23's national-gap diagnostics separately; no exact national reconciliation is added.

Target candidate actuals/winners enter a separate evaluation artifact. Construction reads neither them nor Stage23's party-evaluation actuals/scores. The observed-input A/B reference is conditional on actual target local party support. C/D are conditional on observed target **national** support and retrospective slates, not a genuine pre-election forecast. All elections have been reused in development. Operational selections remain null regardless of the sign of I or any share/winner trade-off.
