## Frozen statistical specification — 2026-09-23

`data/processed/models/replacement-candidate/specification.json` was recorded after the committed inventory and before Stage 10 effect arithmetic. The estimand is predictive target residual difference for a **source-winner replacement**, conditional on the outgoing residual and known target candidacy, not a personal-vote or retirement effect. Equal-weight percentage-point residuals, an intercept, unchanged-boundary general scope, source-anchored identity and separate challenger/Māori inventories are specified there. The natural constant-shift model uses an outgoing residual plus a replacement indicator; a retention interaction would instead ask whether more of the outgoing residual is lost when the candidate changes. Neither can be fitted on the strict cohort because there are zero supported replacement events. This stage will report arithmetic/descriptive diagnostics and an explicit null operational selection. Retrospectively corroborated events may be summarized but cannot determine selection or be called an outcome-independent backtest. No Stage 8/9 descriptive slope or Stage 6 elasticity is imported.

The first specification commit accidentally reused the JSON key `status` for the freeze marker and the outgoing-status definition, leaving only the latter in the file. This key collision was found by the analysis runner's freeze assertion **before any effect calculation**. The content was separated into `freezeStatus` and `outgoingStatusDefinition`; no estimand, eligibility rule, formula or gate changed. The original commit remains in history.

## Final evidence and diagnostics

The full identity rebuild after removing target/later winner flags finds 23 identity-class changes among 1,433 inventory records, all outside the primary cohort. No primary classification or eligibility changes. This establishes flag independence of the 39 supported continuations, not independence of retrospective profile availability. The eight otherwise comparable distinct-person source-winner cases need target/later winner anchors; **all eight incoming candidates won**. They are outcome-selected retrospective illustrations and do not supply a replacement validation cohort. The preserved Stage 9 acquisition was scoped to source-winner recontesters, which further limits incoming and losing-candidate coverage. The primary cohort includes both National and Labour continuations (22 and 17) but zero treated replacements; challenger comparisons are inventory only. Incoming career history is unknown for 1,370/1,433 pairs; 63 have an anchored dated profile. An unknown history is never set to no prior service.
Four of the 39 strict continuations lose the target election (two in 2011 and two in 2023); the rule does not require target victory. Among comparable source-winner party-seat records, 13/21/27 target names differ in the three transitions, but those strings include aliases and unresolved identities and are **not** replacement counts.

No replacement model, no-effect fitted comparator or chronological replacement score is identifiable on the same two-group sample. For transparency, simple **descriptive** additive arithmetic is:

They are simple arithmetic benchmarks, **not** a fitted replacement-versus-no-replacement backtest. The retrospective mean change switches sign in 2014→2017 under proportional (+0.377 pp) and log-odds (+0.168 pp) normalization; 2008→2011 and 2020→2023 remain negative on those scales. Group sizes, one transition with only three supported continuations, shared party/election reference offsets, repeated party-seats and stable seat/party conditions rule out pair-iid significance claims. The observed contrasts cannot separate loss of outgoing overperformance, incoming strength, national swings or retirement selection.

**Operational selection:** `selectedOperationalReplacementEffectPP = null` because the predeclared both-groups-each-transition gate fails. This is an evidence/identification limit, not a fitted zero or a finding that replacement has no effect. The Stage 8 persistence and Stage 9 freshman operational selections remain null. No earlier model output is edited.

## Future integration boundary

A later complete model needs a candidate baseline on the **target boundaries** and explicit candidate identity/status before moving an earlier residual forward. It must replace the outgoing candidate's residual conditionally if evidence supports doing so, never carry that residual unchanged and subtract a separate turnover penalty as well. Incoming history marked unknown cannot be assigned zero strength. Stage 6 unconditional National/Labour elasticity can absorb candidate turnover correlated with party movement; after this null Stage 10 result, a separate conditional-elasticity review remains warranted **before** integration, but cannot use a Stage 10 replacement coefficient that was not identified. Do not refit Stage 6 within Stage 10.
