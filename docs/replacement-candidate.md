# Stage 10 replacement-candidate effects

## Pre-fit inventory checkpoint — 2026-09-23

Stage 9 PR #16 was verified merged as `1f47a7e`; this branch begins there. This inventory was generated **before** Stage 10 effect fitting. It pairs all observed same-party contests in the same named seat under the existing Stage 5 party-continuity contract, rather than starting from Stage 8 returning-person pairs. There are 1,433 pair records across all five adjacent transitions. Source and target candidate occurrence IDs, names, affiliations and residuals remain immutable Stage 7 records. The two changed-boundary transitions are inventoried but excluded from the primary analysis. A matching seat name alone does not override boundary regime.

The event classes are intentionally conservative. A `supported_continuation` has the same Stage 8 person link, an exact name/party/seat chain and an official source-or-earlier winner anchor; the target occurrence remains a **probable** projected link unless directly corroborated. A `supported_replacement` requires distinct official people each anchored **before** the target result. A `retrospective_distinct_people` link can rely on a target or later winner anchor and cannot enter outcome-independent validation. Changed names with the same person link are retained as possible aliases; different strings without corroboration remain unresolved. No first appearance is treated as first candidacy or never served. Departure reason stays unknown without evidence. A source winner is a winner at the preceding general election; this label does not assert that service continued uninterrupted to target day. The Stage 9 dated profile overlay records known prior electorate/list service separately from unknown incoming career history.

The full inventory includes 10, 18, 18, 18 and 17 Māori party-seat pairs by transition. The processed election winner flag used for source incumbent classification covers general electorates only, so Māori outgoing status is **unknown**, not challenger, and Māori evidence remains separate. In the three unchanged-boundary transitions, the pre-result primary sample has 39 general continuations and **zero** confirmed different-person replacements. This does not mean no replacements occurred. It means the preserved official profile corpus cannot establish incoming identities before results across these events. Source profile coverage among comparable general source winners is 41/54, 58/64 and 49/65. The 107 Stage 9 profiles were acquired for source-winner recontesters; their availability and retrospective publication are selected toward MPs and cannot provide balanced evidence about incoming losers. Candidate-name contrasts alone would create a much larger but potentially misclassified and outcome-dependent group.

The inventory keeps challenger transitions separately and never pools them with incumbent replacement. Party entry/exit, missing matching contest, cancelled contests and boundary changes are excluded with reasons. Prior electorate and list service are only populated when a unique, reconciled dated profile is available; unknown is explicit. There was no new raw-source acquisition for this checkpoint. Stage-specific inputs pin Stage 7 occurrences, Stage 8 links, Stage 5 continuity, Stage 9 dated evidence and its contract, and processed elections; the Stage 9 provenance check retains its raw-byte guarantees.
