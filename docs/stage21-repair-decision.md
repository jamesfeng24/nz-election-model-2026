# Stage 21 repair decision — 2026-10-01

This is a supplemental correction and evidence checkpoint. The original Stage 11, Stage 18–20 and identity outputs remain historical artifacts; the [original Stage 11 hash manifest](../data/processed/checkpoints/stage21-stage11-geography-repair/original-artifact-hashes.json), [corrected companion](../data/processed/checkpoints/stage21-stage11-geography-repair/) and [identity pilot ledger](../data/processed/checkpoints/stage21-identity-pilot/) identify the exact changes. No new model was fitted.

## Geographic correction

Stage 11 matched electorate labels literally even though the certified unchanged-boundary ID frame matched eight 2008→2011 seats whose labels gained macrons. The corrected adapter uses that independent ID frame; it does not strip diacritics, infer geography from a number, or match people. It recovers the independently verified **33** supported candidate categories, increasing partial applicability from **247/285/271** to **280/285/271** in the 2011/2017/2023 holdouts (803 to 836 total). All 803 previously applicable prediction fields are unchanged. The 2011 local matched-component MAE interval is **2.244–2.263 pp** on the original 247, **2.530–2.549 pp** on the new 33, and **2.277–2.297 pp** on the corrected 280; corresponding corrected pooled and party-only controls are **3.810–3.828** and **3.937–3.947 pp**. The 2017/2023 results are unchanged. Full published candidate totals lie in transported full-vote bounds for **285/836**, versus 267/803 originally. All 191 comparable target contests still have unmatched party groups. The local matched component continues to beat its controls under the original diagnostic, but it is not a complete candidate forecast; operational split selection remains null. The corrected 2011 Stage 5 sensitivity also retains the observed-national-support/turnout condition. The source-seat geometry, routing, rounding, benchmarks and selection gate were not retuned.

The correction lives alongside the original outputs. Stage 19's diagnostic manifest pins the original Stage 11 artifacts and was not silently regenerated; Stage 20's certified-ID feature inventory and frozen four-model contract are likewise unchanged. Any later use of a Stage 11 score must identify the corrected companion explicitly.

The two appended Stage21 source registrations exposed a separate Stage13 deterministic manifest that still included a whole-registry hash. Its existing immutable source snapshots already pin every required Stage13 registry record and raw byte, and its builder verifies those snapshots. We removed **only** the whole-registry hash from Stage13's manifest inputs. The Stage13 manifest metadata and generator hash changed; its occurrence, person, relation, coverage and conflict artifacts remain byte-identical. A regression test accepts unrelated registrations while existing tests reject missing/changed/ambiguous required records and altered raw bytes. This is a provenance-contract correction, not new Stage13 adjudication.

## Alliance and category evidence

The [preserved official-source overlay](../data/processed/checkpoints/stage21-alliance-mapping/overlay.json) resolves the factual group relationship: the 2014 Internet/MANA and 2023 Freedoms NZ ballots are **shared alliance groups**, not unrelated parties or zero-support affiliations. It has exact election-local candidate, group, vote and source IDs with a 118-record raw-byte source contract. Each of **52** affected held general seats has exactly one standing constituent candidate for the shared group in that seat. Stage 18 excluded 26 held 2014 Internet/MANA training seats and 20 held 2023 Vision target seats; six 2023 Rock the Vote/NZ Outdoors candidates were already constructed as affirmative no-party-group candidates. Those six classifications are wrong as facts. The cancelled Port Waikato Vision nomination remains excluded.

A group-level single-destination route could make the **26+20** excluded seats eligible and correct the six existing inputs without dividing or duplicating party mass. It is an explicit *model representation assumption*: the published group support does not identify constituent-specific party allocation. We did not add these seats to a fitted sample or score them. The overlay supplies exact case IDs for a later pre-fit amendment; any amended Stage 20 sample needs regenerated applicability, common IDs, feature/rank checks and contract **before fitting**. No external alliance queries were needed.

## Fixed identity pilot

The [claim ledger](../data/processed/checkpoints/stage21-identity-pilot/claim-ledger.json) retains **888** comparable same-party source/target cases (297/303/288). Ten inherited distinct-person routes remain in the ledger; the other 878 are unresolved relationship questions under the strict pilot protocol. Exactly **24** were fixed before searching (eight per transition; seven general and one Māori each), with deterministic ambiguity-type and ID strata. The resulting sampling fractions vary by stratum and are not population weights. Winner flags, model errors, residuals, inherited confidence and profile availability did not select or reorder cases.

The execution used **four** collection queries, **48** targeted queries (two per case) and **two** unique new official party biography resources, under ceilings of 8/48/30. The all-888 inherited audit preceded the first collection queries, but the separate 24-case preserved-review file was saved after those four queries; this protocol-sequence difference is retained. No targeted query preceded the selected-case review. Query timestamps are approximate batch windows where exact times were not logged. Every query, including failures, remains in the execution ledger. The two retrieved biographies support **two historical career facts**, with raw SHA-256, exact passages, historical fact intervals and retrieval dates. Their publication dates and pre-target availability are unknown. They do **not** explicitly bridge both exact election-local nominations, so the independently checked result is **0 newly confirmed same-person relations, 0 newly corroborated different-person relations and 24 unresolved relations**. There are no budget-exhausted cases; unused resource/query capacity was not treated as a target. All 48 election-local occurrences remain established by their official candidate records; no new person-link confidence was promoted. No first-ever service or complete career was inferred.

The [coverage report](../data/processed/checkpoints/stage21-identity-pilot/coverage.json) separates transition, general/Māori, party, source/target role and later-observed winner/loser status. Official candidate counts supply outcome diagnostics only after fixed selection; the Māori source-winner calculation agrees with all 21 preserved official winner-overlay records. Changing winner flags changes only these diagnostics, not the chosen cases, searches or adjudication. This computational check does not make retrospective party-biography availability balanced. Prominent people remain easier to document, and the pilot's deliberately unequal ambiguity-stratum fractions prevent treating its unweighted yield as representative.

The fixed search route did not demonstrate a scalable way to close exact relationship gaps. **Do not scale this source strategy automatically** or treat unresolved as replacement. A future identity programme would need a new, balanced source route and separate authorization. Broad identity repair is not a prerequisite for the identity-free Stage 20 S/V experiment.

## Next decision

Stage 20's four-model fit should **not** run against the unchanged original predictor file because six constructed contests contain known factual no-party-group misclassifications. The smallest next bounded implementation is a **pre-fit group-level mapping amendment** using the exact Stage 21 overlay, with explicit single-destination routing, all 52 affected IDs, regenerated common samples/applicability and within-slate rank checks, and no target outcomes in eligibility. If the frozen gates still pass, fit only Stage 20's four jointly refitted restrictions under the amended contract; keep the original contract and sample as historical checkpoints. If the group representation is not adopted, exclude the six erroneous constructed cases and rerun the same gates before fitting on the reduced sample. The amended sample choice must be made before inspecting new model scores. Stage 11 correction and identity pilot do not authorize that fit here. Family A, complete party-vector construction, as-of forecasting, integration and ensemble weights remain separate work.
