## Join and ballot meaning

The fixed Stage 14 frame supplies independently validated source/target electorate IDs and unchanged-boundary regimes. Stage 18 supplies the full election-local target slate and conservative party-ballot mapping. A target registered party category is linked to a source party category only by the unique eligible Stage 5 continuity record. The preserved Stage 11 source local split matrix then supplies an **exact source party-ballot row count** and a **two-decimal rounded percentage** in the column of the unique source same-party candidate. `S0` is this historical party-row-to-candidate-destination proportion. `V0=C0−P0` uses the source candidate's votes divided by valid **candidate** votes and source party votes divided by valid **party** votes. These are distinct denominators; `V0` is a source party-seat gap, not candidate competitiveness, identified personal strength or evidence that the target candidate is the same person. Names, source/target win flags and target candidate results never determine a feature.

The printed `S0` value is an explicit future **working approximation**, not an exact observed conditional probability. The ledger also keeps the exact coupled range for that one selected cell after all of its row's rounding bounds and sum-to-one constraint. A deterministic lower or upper witness can complete every published row to 100%; endpoints are never independently combined from incompatible columns. A printed `0.00` on a positive row remains rounded observed evidence. A zero-mass party origin does not establish `S0`, while earlier candidate/party totals can still establish `V0`. No zero-mass source row occurred among the supported historical target candidates in this frame.

Supported S/V evidence is official **source-election** local split and candidate/party totals. The source-election results are historical facts before the target election, but the preserved source registrations generally give **retrieval in 2026**, not demonstrated publication by the historical nomination-close cutoff. The target local party vector and target slate are retrospective conditional inputs. This audit therefore supports an outcome-blind *computational* feature construction under fixed preserved evidence, not a verified historical as-of information set. Target candidate totals and split destinations remain evaluation-only in any later authorized study.

## Fitting-gate and identifiability audit

The proposed common constructed samples are exact Stage 18 IDs plus the source feature join: 2017 trains on 63 complete 2011 contests/423 candidates and evaluates 64/431; 2023 trains on 127 completed 2011+2017 contests/854 candidates and evaluates 44/312. The earliest 2011 holdout has no earlier eligible fitted training transition and remains parameter-free benchmark-only. In the 2017 training set 280 candidates in all 63 contests have S; its holdout has 285 in all 64. In the 2023 training set 565 candidates in all 127 contests have S; its holdout has 192 in all 44. **Both predeclared minimum gates—20 complete contests and 20 with supported S, in both training and evaluation—pass.** No outcome was examined to set or relax a threshold.

Equal-contest, within-slate candidate-weighted supported-feature means from the **earlier training contests only** are `μS=0.5631932252, μV=0.0097081366` for 2017 and `μS=0.5846963088, μV=0.0127367938` for 2023. Within each training slate, S and V both vary in every complete contest once the declared neutral fallback is represented explicitly. Their within-slate centered columns have rank **2**; including the floor derivative has rank **3** at fixed, outcome-free `κ` probes 0.0001, 0.01 and 0.1. Normalized-column condition ratios range **1.69–2.79** across the two training folds; S/V centered-column correlations are **0.480/0.523**. The evaluation slates also have rank 2/3 at these probes. These checks show the proposed terms are not algebraically redundant on these inputs. They do not establish stable coefficients, causal identification, predictive improvement or an as-of forecast.

## A prior applicability gap kept separate

Stage 11's historical applicability adapter used an exact source/target **seat-name** join. The independently validated frame joins eight 2008→2011 seats whose recorded names gained macrons (Kaikōura, Māngere, Ōhariu, Ōtaki, Rangitīkei, Tāmaki, Taupō and Te Atatū). That recovers **33 supported 2011 target candidate categories** compared with Stage 11's 247 partial-applicable candidates; this Stage 20 join has 280. The evidence is the same preserved source records, and the frame supplies the geography proof. This is a Stage 11 coverage defect in its old applicability logic, **not** a retroactive change to its saved predictions or matched-component scores. A separately scoped correction would need to rebuild Stage 11 applicability and honestly re-evaluate its matched-component result before using that result as evidence for model selection. Stage 20 does not overwrite Stage 11 or infer person identity from the changed seat label.

**Conclusion:** the fixed Family B applicability and algebraic design-rank gates pass on the preserved conditional frame. The model could be fit **only on separate authorization** under the [final pre-fit contract](stage20-complete-share-prefit-contract.md). Material limitations remain: 409 neutral-fallback candidates on constructed slates, 20 held 2023 mapping abstentions, no comparable Māori route, retrospective party/slate inputs, unknown source publication timing, party-level transfer rather than person linkage, only three development transitions, and the Stage 11 applicability correction dependency above. Operational selection stays null.
