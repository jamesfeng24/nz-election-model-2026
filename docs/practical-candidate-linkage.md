## Rules and evidence labels

The universe contains all **3,007** immutable Stage7 occurrences, including **2,998 held** and **nine cancelled** nominations. All five adjacent transitions are derived from Stage25 geography. Every one of its 356 target seats remains in the coverage ledger, with all predecessors referenced. Primary relationships require its certified two-sided exact predecessor pair and held candidature. There are 246 exact general targets (one cancelled) and 28 exact Māori targets; 75 general and seven Māori targets are geographically excluded. Geography does not establish voter continuity or personal identity.

Official labels use an explicit surname-comma-given order. The whole left segment is the surname, including compounds; the first right token is the substantive given name, with ordered middle tokens. NFC/case/whitespace and specified apostrophe/hyphen glyphs are normalized; accents and original Unicode labels are retained. Unfamiliar spellings, parenthesized tokens, initial-only given names, expanded middle-name conflicts and ambiguous order remain exceptions. A documented alternative name order would require an explicit evidence route; tokens are not silently rearranged.

Automatic acceptance requires compatible names, supported single-party continuity (or explicitly independent-to-independent context), exact geographic context and no competing identity or contradiction. Approved nicknames and omitted/compatible initialed middle names require no biography. Shared ballot-group membership is not constituent-party continuity. Party/seat changes remain explicit exception leads outside automatic same-seat acceptance.

Every compatible name is checked against the **election-wide** occurrence universe, regardless of party, seat, geography or inherited confidence. Identical duplicate representations are deduplicated; conflicting copies fail. Connected components are checked pairwise for simultaneous occurrences, incompatible names, competing identities and rejected internal edges before provisional IDs are assigned. A transitive path cannot bypass a rejected edge. IDs are derived from immutable members and never overwrite historical person IDs.

Labels remain separate:

- `accepted_algorithmic_same_person`: a deterministic research judgment with exact subrule flags, not documentary confirmation.
- `documentary_same_person`: requires an explicit occurrence-specific relationship bridge. **None** was promoted in this pass.
- `documentary_distinct_people`: **ten** independently anchored Stage10 claims, carried with their existing passages, dates and selective retrospective acquisition caveats; no new distinct-person adjudication.
- `unresolved_ambiguous`: includes nonmatches and unsupported context. Different names or provisional IDs do not establish replacement.

Person existence and inherited career evidence are retained in an audit layer, with no promotion from inherited confidence, winner-chain projection or profile existence. Fact years, source publication/retrieval metadata and unknown availability remain separate. Algorithmic adjudication is retrospective; publication by the historical forecast cutoff is **unknown**. A link does not establish complete tenure or first-ever service.
