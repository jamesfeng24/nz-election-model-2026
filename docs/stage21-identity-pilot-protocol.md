# Stage 21 targeted relationship pilot — frozen before external queries

The [machine claim ledger](../data/processed/checkpoints/stage21-identity-pilot/claim-ledger.json) retains **all 888** observed same-party candidate pairs in the three certified unchanged-boundary, held source/target transitions: 297 in 2008→2011, 303 in 2014→2017 and 288 in 2020→2023. It joins election-local Stage 7 occurrence IDs through the independently validated Stage 14 electorate-ID frame and eligible Stage 5 party continuity. The older Stage 10 name-based join missed some 2011 cases; its evidence is attached as a separate inherited channel, not used to create geography. Ten pairs have a preserved Stage 10 distinct-person route and stay in the ledger outside this unresolved pilot. The other **878 relationship questions** remain eligible for selection. Source and target official records, labels, person-existence leads, occurrence-link tiers, relation leads and career completeness are separate. A lexical name category is a **question**, not an adjudication.

The [fixed acquisition plan](../data/processed/checkpoints/stage21-identity-pilot/acquisition-plan.json) chooses **exactly 24** unresolved relationship cases, eight per transition. Within each transition it selects three exact-label general, two name-variant general, two different-label general and one different-label Māori cases. A name variant means only a normalized spelling or common surname, not a proven alias. Each stratum is ordered by ascending SHA-256 of `nz-election-model-2026-stage21-relationship-pilot-v1|caseId`, with the case ID as tie-break. The selected ID list and empty [search ledger](../data/processed/checkpoints/stage21-identity-pilot/search-ledger.json) are pinned before any query. Winners, residuals, model errors, profile availability and inherited confidence **do not rank cases**. Screening an already documented distinct-person relationship out of an *unresolved* pilot is distinct from prioritizing by profile availability; the full ledger retains it. Winner/loser and party composition are reported only after selection. No difficult case can be replaced.

The same source hierarchy and rules apply to all 24: official candidature confirms each local occurrence; a relation needs a source explicitly bridging the two candidacies, an authoritative prior-name bridge, or independently corroborated distinct people. Official Electoral Commission/Parliament history and dated party-authored candidacy/selection statements take precedence; a clearly attributed contemporary archive can preserve an original statement. A result snippet, matching name, single nomination, candidate-submitted profile or later journalist interview alone cannot confirm a cross-election link. Retrospective official evidence can corroborate a historical relationship or service interval, but its publication/retrieval date remains separate from the historical fact and unknown pre-target availability stays unknown. Prior list and electorate service, complete career history and occurrence linkage must never be conflated.

The frozen budget is **at most eight collection-level discovery queries, at most two targeted queries per selected case and at most 30 new unique preserved resources**. All searches, including endpoint tests and failures, must be logged. Reused canonical URLs or identical raw checksums count as one resource; a shared document can support more than one selected case only through separate case-specific passage checks. Failed retrievals consume a query but not a resource slot. Stop at the relevant limit, with unresolved and unreached states explicit; unused capacity is not a target. Nonselected leads remain outside the pilot denominator. The saved plan includes exact query templates, source hierarchy, adjudication and date rules. It authorizes one bounded pass only. No effect fitting, prior adjudication rewrite or expansion follows automatically.

Before external acquisition, the generator and five focused tests verify the 888-case frame, 24 fixed selected IDs, duplicate/ambiguous join rejection, empty search ledger and invariance of selection to winner flags, residuals and inherited identity-confidence changes. Reproduce with `.venv/bin/python -m scripts.checkpoints.stage21_identity_pilot --check`. The manifest pins the processed source inputs, generator and all three pre-acquisition outputs. Search and adjudication outputs will be supplemental records with their own provenance and checksums.

## Preserved review and first collection batch

The [separate selected-case preserved review](../data/processed/checkpoints/stage21-identity-pilot/preserved-review.json) rechecks Stage8 links, Stage10 occurrence routes and dated Stage9 profiles, plus Stage13's primary occurrence/relation records, for all 24 fixed cases. Eight have a directly associated preserved retrospective Parliament profile lead; none of those profiles alone explicitly connects both exact election-local nominations. The prior Stage10 `supported_continuation` label in two selected exact-name cases rests on a source anchor and projected exact-name target chain. Both stay unresolved under the pilot's stricter exact-relation question. Historical service rows are retained separately without declaring full career coverage.

The first **four** collection-level discovery queries were issued after the pre-acquisition plan commit and its all-case inherited-evidence audit, but **before this separately saved selected-case review**. They returned official candidature/profile indexes, not an accepted relationship bridge or a new resource. The [execution ledger](../data/processed/checkpoints/stage21-identity-pilot/search-attempts.json) records the exact queries, approximate batch-minute timestamp, leads and rejection rationale. This sequence difference is explicit; no selected-case targeted query was made before the saved review. The four queries count against the eight-query collection cap and will not be repeated. Search snippets were not promoted to evidence.
