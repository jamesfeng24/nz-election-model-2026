# Proposed Stage 20 experiments after Stage 19 diagnosis

**Status: proposal awaiting separate authorization.** No equation here has been fitted or scored in Stage 19. The fixed diagnostic [inventory](../data/processed/checkpoints/model-failure-diagnostics/diagnostic-inventory.json), [plan](model-failure-diagnostic-plan.md), [paired summaries](model-failure-diagnostics.md) and [machine-readable proposal](../data/processed/checkpoints/model-failure-diagnostics/proposed-experiments.json) distinguish established constraints from later implementation choices. All three comparable elections have already informed development. Chronological testing is useful but cannot be called untouched confirmation or yield three new independent replications by multiplying seats.

## Family A: bounded NAT/LAB conditional party-seat response

**Question.** Does a bounded response to local party movement and/or the independently known source party-seat victory improve next candidate share, on the same unchanged-boundary NAT/LAB party-seat frame, beyond a simple baseline? This is a party-seat association, not an incumbent or candidate-person effect, and does not allocate a complete candidate slate.

Let `C0,C1` be source/target shares of valid candidate votes, `P0,P1` source/target shares of valid party votes, `W0` the source party-seat victory flag, and `L(x)=log(x/(1−x))`. For strictly interior shares, use the one prespecified bounded family

`L(Ĉ1) = L(C0) + α + β [L(P1)−L(P0)] + γ W0`.

Fit the nested restrictions **baseline** (`α` only), **A** (`α,β`), **B** (`α,γ`), and **A+B** (`α,β,γ`) separately by party on identical earlier-election records, with equal party-seat weight and squared error in **candidate-share percentage points** as the training loss. Coefficients are fitted jointly for each restriction; no Stage 6/16 coefficient is imported. The inverse-logit yields an interior candidate-share prediction but does not assert a binomial independent-ballot likelihood. No interaction is initially justified by the three election clusters. This is a **link sensitivity**, not evidence that observed curvature uniquely calls for a logit.

Training/holdout chronology is the Stage 16 unchanged-boundary general 63/64/64 party-seat frame: 2011 benchmark-only, 2017 trained on the 2011 targets, 2023 on 2011+2017. Source and target party movement is the observed-local conditional input for the primary diagnostic. The saved Stage 16 common-intercept and nuisance-matched controls, one-for-one and no-response are reported on the **same aligned party-seat IDs** as external contextual controls. Refit each nested restriction on precisely the same training sample and evaluate on precisely the same target IDs; a paired ablation compares A+B to A and to B, and A/B individually to the baseline. Do not select a term merely because its single-term ablation fails if a substantive joint hypothesis was predeclared. Stage 5 additive/proportional/log-odds are fixed input sensitivities conditional on **observed target national support**; retain their original joint validity/missingness and report boundary abstentions rather than pseudocounting logits. Every candidate/party share must be strictly between zero and one; otherwise the entire method abstains for that party-seat and the common comparison subset is reduced with coverage shown. No clip or smoothing constant is introduced after seeing results.

Budget: at most three mean parameters per party and no tuning grid, shrinkage search or post-hoc interaction. Fit only if each training fold has the prespecified full rank and at least 20 usable party-seats; otherwise abstain and report. Metrics: party/holdout paired MAE, RMSE, signed bias, raw out-of-range status of contextual linear controls, coverage and the fixed Stage 19 support/movement strata. Demand a predeclared ≥0.25 pp MAE gain over the nested baseline in **both** trained folds, no ≥0.25 pp RMSE regression in either, no loss of coverage relative to the support gates, and no sign reversal of the practical gain across retained valid Stage 5 input modes before calling the association worth further validation. This screen is not an operational coefficient rule. Stop if gains reverse or are immaterial; no retuning of link, bounds or groups on these elections. A constrained complete-slate embedding and a target-boundary candidate baseline would still be required before forecast use.

## Family B: identity-free complete-share lagged split and party-seat viability

**Question.** Do **party-level**, previous-seat splitting and prior party-seat candidate/party gap help allocate a complete target valid-candidate-share slate beyond a neutral party-support composition? Stage 11's local pattern helped its *matched party-ballot component*, but that is not a complete candidate outcome. This family tests a coherent complete-share outcome while keeping the evidence tier explicit. `S0` is the previous held general election's reported same-party-row-to-same-party-candidate-category split proportion for an exact continuing party category on comparable geography; its published two-decimal rounding is retained as measurement uncertainty. `V0=C0−P0` is the prior **party-seat** candidate/party gap, with distinct valid-vote denominators. Neither quantity is claimed to be an identified personal effect or automatically transferable to a replacement.

For a complete, uniquely mapped target slate and supplied observed target local valid-party shares, define baseline intensity `b_c = P1,g(c)+κ` for mapped candidates and `b_c=κ` for affirmatively no-party-group candidates. A supported party candidate with both lagged features has

`w_c = b_c × exp(θS × (S0,g(c)−μS) + θV × (V0,g(c)−μV))`; `m_c=w_c/Σ_d w_d`.

`μS,μV` are simple means calculated on that fold's earlier **training** records only, never the holdout. For a candidate lacking a supported prior party category, the adjustment is omitted and its intensity remains `b_c`; this is a declared neutral fallback, **not a finding of zero personal strength**. Missing-feature and no-party-group categories are reported separately. Unknown person identity does not change mapping or eligibility. If party/category mapping is ambiguous, source split table is missing, target party vector is incomplete, or the slate is incomplete, the full contest abstains. A source row with zero mass cannot establish `S0`; printed zero is not a missing row. Source category entry/exit is not silently linked. A candidate-specific source split column is never transferred by assuming the target person is the same.

Compare four jointly refitted restrictions on an identical holdout sample: baseline (`κ`), baseline+S (`κ,θS`), baseline+V (`κ,θV`) and baseline+S+V (`κ,θS,θV`). There is no interaction in the primary family. Fit the single positive floor and all included terms **jointly** by equal-contest mean cross entropy with `κ` in the Stage 18 prespecified `[0.0001,0.1]` party-share-unit range; do not insert Stage 18's separately fitted κ. No additional categories, free candidate intercepts, transformed floors or hyperparameter search. Uniform is a parameter-free context control; the zero-floor party-proportional rule remains restricted to its supported subset. A fit family with missing lagged features is evaluated as one declared fallback model; separately report the feature-complete subset to show how much gain comes from observed features versus fallbacks, without selecting the more attractive subset. Use all eligible earlier unchanged-boundary target contests with an earlier comparable source matrix for training; 2011 is benchmark-only, 2017 trains on 2011, and 2023 on 2011+2017. The common evaluation frame is the fixed Stage 18 213-contest frame, with Māori/cancelled/ambiguous mapping abstentions retained. Earlier 2008, 2014 and 2020 contests without a validated lagged comparable source are **not** silently inserted into this family merely because Stage 18 used them to fit κ.

Before any fit, an authorized implementation must produce a deterministic **applicability-only** audit of the exact Stage 11 source rows joined to Stage 18 election-local party/category records: source/target record IDs, rounding intervals, positive source mass, source candidate destination support, unchanged-boundary key, feature missingness, party entry/exit and all target slates. Pin the resulting common sample and source records. If either trained holdout has fewer than 20 complete constructed contests or fewer than 20 contests with a supported `S0` feature, **stop without fitting this family** and report evidence insufficiency. Do not substitute a different feature or choose a favorable partial cohort. For reported scores, use Stage 18's contest-equal MAE/RMSE, candidate-equal sensitivity, category signed bias, unique-winner/tie counts and full-frame coverage. An incremental association must gain ≥0.25 pp contest-equal MAE over its jointly refitted baseline in both trained folds without ≥0.25 pp RMSE regression or coverage loss; it must also survive a predeclared rounding-endpoint sensitivity and not depend solely on a few influential contests. Those thresholds screen whether another validation design is worth discussing; they cannot make these development elections confirmatory or choose an operational candidate model.

## Sequence, blocked inputs and stopping

The recommended next **bounded implementation**, after review and separate authorization, is **Family B's applicability audit followed by its four prespecified conditional complete-share ablations only if the coverage gates pass**. It directly tests whether Stage 11's useful matched-component signal improves the Stage 18 complete-share outcome, without inventing a candidate-person link. Files would be one stage-specific source/feature inventory, frozen common-sample contract, construction/prediction and separate evaluation artifacts, tests and an unmerged PR. No source acquisition is needed. Stop before fitting if the feature join or coverage gate fails; stop after the four ablations if improvement is unstable or immaterial. Family A is a second, separate authorization candidate, not an automatic follow-on or alternative chosen by looking at Family B's scores. Neither family changes current operational nulls.

The testing ladder remains: (1) local-party diagnostics conditional on actual national support; (2) candidate diagnostics conditional on actual local party support; (3) complete-pipeline diagnostics from actual national support through a **coherent full local party-category vector** into candidate predictions; (4) genuine historical as-of forecasts using dated pre-result national/party/slate/turnout inputs; (5) validated joint uncertainty and only then operational integration or ensemble decisions. Stage 18 cannot run step 3 today: every retained Stage 5 substitution lacks a complete coherent party vector for its constructed slates. A separately scoped full-vector design is needed before step 3; zero-filling missing categories, stitching marginal intervals or renormalizing unrelated predictions is prohibited. Dated as-of inputs and turnout are not prerequisites for steps 1–2, but are required to call step 4 a forecast. Stage 13's 19 confirmed occurrence claims and no primary pre-result cross-election relations do not presently support broad same-person persistence in these families. A future shrunken residual or joint variance model requires its own balanced identity or uncertainty-evidence gate; failed mean terms alone are not a reason to widen uncertainty.
