# Deferred dated adjustment and Māori measurement interfaces

Stage46 implements neither interface and applies no historical override. All surprising outcomes remain in the unadjusted residual and evaluation samples.

A future manual adjustment record must retain the unadjusted forecast, affected seat/boundary/candidate IDs, dated evidence available by the forecast cutoff, author, rationale, expiry/review date, and exact log-intensity/share or uncertainty change. The adjusted complete slate must normalize coherently; raw and adjusted outputs both persist. Unknown exceptional circumstances remain part of ordinary predictive uncertainty. Improved known historical outcomes do not validate manual judgment.

Māori electorate polls are a separate measurement layer alongside an explicit Māori-seat baseline and unpolled/stale-seat fallback. National Te Pāti Māori party support is distinct from local candidate support. Preserve the actual candidate/local-party question, denominator, fieldwork/publication dates, sample/mode, boundary and candidate mapping, error and dependence. Polls are neither exact outcomes nor arbitrary manual overrides. Do not duplicate evidence already used by national or other inputs. General-seat uncertainty does not automatically apply to Māori seats.

Remaining bounded work: official nomination/slate refresh; separately designed Māori baseline/polling; reconciliation with defensible turnout/denominator weights; MMP/live assembly and dated forecast archives. No source acquisition, poll fitting, override or live probability follows from this document.
