# Stage 19: frozen-model failure diagnostics

This is an exploratory reading of **saved predictions**, not a refit or an operational selection. The [pre-summary plan](model-failure-diagnostic-plan.md) and [pinned artifact inventory](../data/processed/checkpoints/model-failure-diagnostics/diagnostic-inventory.json) were committed before these summaries. The machine-readable [paired rows and fixed summaries](../data/processed/checkpoints/model-failure-diagnostics/paired-diagnostics.json) retain exact IDs, actuals, controls, units, denominators, information sets, fixed strata, sparse-group counts and the five largest paired changes in each fold. No source, earlier prediction, eligibility or operational null was changed.

For every aligned point observation, signed error is prediction minus actual in percentage points (pp). The reported paired change is model absolute error minus control absolute error; negative is improvement. Squared-error changes are in pp². Stage 18 reports both candidate-equal and contest-equal arithmetic. Stage 6/16 observations are one party-seat each. No result below is a candidate-iid significance test: only three comparable election transitions exist and all have informed model development.

The Stage 18 zero-floor control is defined only when all standing candidates have positive matched party support and no no-party-group candidate. Its 93 contests are therefore a selected **applicability** subset, shown alongside the full 213-contest frame: 191 held general, 21 Māori coverage-only and one cancelled. Of the 191 held general, 171 contests/1,166 candidates construct and 20 contests/147 candidates abstain on ambiguous 2023 party mapping. The nine cancelled Port Waikato nominations are outside the 1,313 held-general candidates. The restricted comparison covers 43/31/19 contests; it cannot diagnose the floor's effect for independents. The full-slate uniform control covers 63/64/44 constructed contests, but its large disadvantage mainly reflects withholding supplied local party support.

On Stage 18's full constructed sample the floor beats uniform by 13.80/13.99/11.25 pp contest-equal MAE in 2011/2017/2023. That does **not** isolate κ. The incremental trained-fold gains against zero-floor are below the frozen 0.25 pp practical screen. With a fixed slate and party vector, adding a common κ never changes candidate ranking. The restricted comparison contains no no-party-group candidates. On the wider constructed sample, the previously saved independent-candidate signed biases are −0.160/−0.857/−0.892 pp: positive neutral mass does not explain their individual strength. The floor moves many tiny predicted shares toward actuals but can worsen larger candidates; fixed control-share bins and influential IDs in the paired file make that trade-off visible. For example, the 2023 restricted control-share bin below 2% has 28 candidates and mean paired improvement −0.354 pp, while the 2017 5–10% bin has 34 candidates and worsens by +0.097 pp. These are development subgroups, not independent confirmation or a reason to retune κ. Party/category biases matter; full-slate overall signed bias is zero by construction.

Labour Stage 16 changes sign across holdouts. In the observed-local input, 48/64 Labour 2017 seats have party movement above +10 pp and 64/64 Labour 2023 seats below −10 pp. The source-victory term improves source-loser rows but worsens source-winner rows in both folds (2017: −2.167 versus +1.457 pp; 2023: −0.447 versus +1.269 pp). These strata describe different party environments and baseline selection; source victory does not establish target personal incumbency. National's 2017 gain is effectively zero and its 2023 gain is below 0.25 pp. The Stage 5 transformed-input sensitivity keeps Labour's 2017 improvement and 2023 worsening across additive, proportional and log-odds; National remains small. Observed target party movement is a **conditional diagnostic input**, not an as-of forecast variable. The fixed scatterplots show these paired patterns without smoothing: [Stage 18 floor](../data/processed/checkpoints/model-failure-diagnostics/stage18-floor-vs-zero.png), [Stage 16 party movement](../data/processed/checkpoints/model-failure-diagnostics/stage16-party-movement.png), and [Stage 16 source gap](../data/processed/checkpoints/model-failure-diagnostics/stage16-source-gap.png). Apparent curvature does not identify a logit law. A source candidate-party gap is not an identified personal effect.

Influence is reported, never used to drop an observation. On the restricted Stage 18 comparison, `nz-general-2011-electorate-22-candidate-07` worsens by **+1.19 pp** in absolute error, while `nz-general-2017-electorate-03-candidate-04` improves by **−0.95 pp**. For Stage 16 Labour 2017, `2014-2017:nz-general-2017-electorate-30:labourparty` improves by **−2.49 pp** relative to common-intercept. The top five per fold and every paired error remain in the diagnostic dataset; a few large changes cannot be interpreted as election-level replication or a causal local effect.

The Stage 6 Labour zero-intercept fit improves sharply in 2017 and fails in 2023 against one-for-one, while National's mean absolute error worsens in both. Stage 6 and Stage 16 have different intercept/status restrictions; their columns must not be read as one model search. Some Stage 16 raw control predictions fall outside [0,1], which the diagnostic preserves in explicit out-of-range bins rather than clipping. Stage 8–10 aggregate-only findings remain as corrected in their stage documents; manufacturing per-event fits would break this checkpoint's no-new-fitting boundary. Stage 11's local matched component beat its controls in all three elections, but 267/803 published candidate totals fell inside the transported full-vote ranges, so unmatched mass and transfer remain material. Stage 15's wide containing intervals likewise do not demonstrate predictive skill.

## Bounded conclusion

The current evidence supports **at most two later experiment families**: a bounded NAT/LAB conditional response test and an identity-free complete-share test of lagged party-level split behaviour with prior party-seat viability. The [proposed experiment contract](model-failure-experiment-specification.md) freezes their equations, common samples and stopping gates for review; it does not fit them. A same-person residual test stays deferred until balanced identity evidence can support it; a variance model is not licensed merely by weak mean results. The Stage 18 floor need not be revisited as a standalone effect. Stage 5 transform choice and every existing null operational selection remain unchanged.

Stage 18 currently cannot support a level-three complete pipeline from observed national support because retained Stage 5 transforms do not provide a complete coherent target party-category vector for any constructed contest. That is a distinct party-vector design dependency; zero-filling, stitching marginal intervals or independent renormalization is not a repair. As-of slates, party support and turnout, a defensible 2026 target-boundary candidate baseline, and joint uncertainty remain later dependencies. All 2011/2017/2023 outcomes are development evidence. No nested re-use of their seats creates a new untouched election.
