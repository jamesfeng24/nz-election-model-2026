## One parsimonious joint model

For K active categories choose a fixed orthonormal Helmert matrix H (K×(K−1), columns orthogonal to1). Use the frozen schema order: column j (j=1,…,K−1) has 1/sqrt(j(j+1)) in its first j rows, −j/sqrt(j(j+1)) in row j+1 and zeros elsewhere. These coordinates avoid privileging National or an arbitrary Other reference. Weekly grid starts at previous completed election, with exact final cutoff node and partial-week elapsed fractions. No latent node informed by a future poll. Latent election-intention support:

`pi_t=softmax(H x_t)`

`x_(t+1)=x_t + sqrt(delta_days/7) sigma z_t`, `z_t~Normal(0,I)`.

One shared weekly scale `sigma~HalfNormal(0.035)` in orthonormal log-share units. Isotropic coordinate innovations imply compositional negative/positive dependence after transformation; fixed isotropy is the strong regularization replacing a poorly identified high-dimensional movement covariance. No campaign multiplier, mean reversion, fundamentals, heavy tails or alternate covariance tournament.

Persistent pollster/party contrasts: `h_a=tau_h(u_a−mean_a u_a)`, `u_a~Normal(0,I)`, `tau_h~HalfNormal(0.12)`. Center equally across the fixed admitted pollster roster for the training window; no frequency weighting. A known methodology segment adds `d_segment~Normal(0,0.05² I)`, base segment0; the inherited Reid January2017 break is explicitly **assumed dated**, not verified. Undocumented changes are flags, not fitted breakpoint searches. No term-varying house effects.

Common cycle polling offset `b_cycle~Normal(0,tau_b² I)`, `tau_b~HalfNormal(0.08)`. A fresh current-cycle b persists across all polls and forecast draws. Earlier completed cycles/results inform its scale; current polls cannot identify shared bias independently of truth. House sum constraint separates relative house effects from common error; completed results and the diffusion prior provide the remaining information. Few elections leave tau_b prior-sensitive; report its prior/posterior contribution, never let many polls erase it. No unrestricted bias covariance or party-specific scale.

Poll expectation is the fieldwork-weighted mean of `softmax(H(x_t+h_a+d_segment+b_cycle))`. For a category/group probability mu, sampling variance is `2*mu*(1−mu)/n_decided`. Add independent nonsampling variance **0.005²** on share scale (fixed approximation, avoids pretending nominal n fully determines error). The likelihood is the product of Normal interval probabilities over reported cells at their rounding/censoring intervals. Compute log CDF differences stably. This is a declared conditional-independent Gaussian sampling/reporting approximation, **not a multinomial count likelihood**; covariance of published sampling errors is omitted. Joint political support/dependence/common bias enter through latent states and contrasts. Report this approximation as a limitation; no silent use of fake integer counts. Missing observations contribute1; structurally absent categories outside the cycle schema have no coordinate. Full-row rounding constraints are checked for feasibility before ingestion; no independent incompatible endpoint scenarios are fitted. A reported exclusive aggregate uses its summed mu once, not duplicated subcomponents.

Initial previous-election support uses full preserved national valid-party votes, including Māori/ordinary/special votes, not selected seats. Continuing categories' observed shares set the initial state. For a genuinely entering modeled category such as TOP with no previous ballot, allocate a **prior mean0.002** from Other proportionally, and assign its new contrast SD1.0; this is a visible entrant prior, not observed support or a generic data pseudocount. Precisely, form the seeded simplex s0, with each entrant's0.002 subtracted from Other, then set x0 mean=Hᵀlog(s0), covariance=Hᵀdiag(v)H, where v=1.0² for entrants and0.01² for continuing categories. This is a category-log perturbation projected into the fixed coordinates, rather than attaching an entrant variance to an arbitrary Helmert column. The frozen major-party starting counts are positive; an unsupported zero continuing modeled category triggers construction abstention instead of an undeclared seed. If prior Other cannot supply entry mass, abstain at construction. Exited minor groups stay within Other; no new fine-grained allocation. The exact previous election's zero count is not a zero forever. Completed earlier cycle endpoint shares are measurement anchors with Normal share SD0.0005, not held-out result conditioning; rebuild each fit using only permitted earlier snapshots. This tiny fixed variance is a numerical anchoring approximation, not measured electoral uncertainty.
