# Stage 18 conditional candidate-share implementation specification

**Pre-fit checkpoint.** This fixes computational choices after the committed [mapping inventory](conditional-candidate-share-inventory.md) and before any Stage 18 parameter or score calculation. Stage 17's reviewed model and exclusions remain unchanged. These historical comparisons are development diagnostics conditional on observed target local party support and retrospective candidacy, never as-of forecasts.

## Inputs, frame and chronology

Use the fixed 213 transition contests: 191 held general targets (63/64/64), 21 Māori coverage-only targets and cancelled 2023 Port Waikato. The expected held-general slate is 423/431/459 candidates (1,313). Admit a held general target only with complete, unique election-local party-group classification and a complete valid-party vector. A mapped candidate uses the exact published local party group; an affirmative independent/unregistered no-group candidate has base support zero. Report-only Internet MANA (2014) and Freedoms NZ/Vision NZ (2023) links remain ambiguous, not zero or guessed party support. Preserve every candidate in an admitted slate, regardless of votes, elected status, identity, presumed replacement or profile availability.

All held general seats in completed earlier elections are training candidates, not only seats in the unchanged-boundary evaluation frame. Exclude an earlier seat only for cancellation, incompatible ballot totals or ambiguous mapping. Target 2011 trains on **2008**; target 2017 on **2008, 2011, 2014**; target 2023 on **2008, 2011, 2014, 2017, 2020**. Require at least 20 complete general contests and five containing an independent or explicitly zero-support candidate. Target candidate votes, winner flags, residuals, identities and split destinations never determine that target's inputs or its fitted parameter. Earlier completed candidate outcomes may fit later floors.

## Equation and deterministic optimizer

For a complete party-share vector `p`, standing slate `C`, and floor `κ`, set `b_c=p[party(c)]` for exact mapped candidates, `b_c=0` only for affirmative no-group candidates. Predict `m_c=(b_c+κ)/(Σ_d b_d+|C|κ)`. The one shared floor is fitted over **[0.0001, 0.1]** in party-share units to minimize the equal-contest mean of `−Σ_c s_c log(m_c)` where `s_c` is an earlier completed valid-candidate share. Use SciPy SHGO deterministic 1D global search with 256 Sobol sample points and two iterations, `f_tol=1e-12`, followed by explicit evaluation of both interval endpoints and all returned local minima. Choose the lowest objective, breaking objective ties within `1e-12` toward the smaller κ. Independently check a 4,097-point uniform grid plus bounded local refinement around every sampled local minimum; require objective agreement within `1e-8` and parameter agreement within `1e-4` except a flat/tied optimum, which is reported. Check successful solver status, finite objective, range and explicit boundary classification within `1e-7`. Failure is a fit abstention, not an arbitrary fallback. The objective is not assumed globally convex. Do not expand the range after seeing scores.

## Methods and metrics

Compare fitted floor and parameter-free uniform `1/|C|` on identical all-supported target contests. The **restricted** zero-floor rule is `b_c/Σ b` only when *every* candidate maps to a registered group with strictly positive local support. It is excluded from no-group/zero-support contests; report its full-frame applicability, then evaluate all three methods on this identical restricted subset. The fitted floor and uniform also retain their broader shared subset. No comparator is broadened after scoring.

Within a contest, candidate-share MAE is `100 × mean_c |m_c−s_c|` and MSE is `10,000 × mean_c (m_c−s_c)^2`. Contest-equal MAE is the mean contest MAE; contest-equal RMSE is **the square root after averaging contest MSE**, in percentage points. Candidate-equal sensitivity takes one mean across all candidate absolute/squared errors, with its RMSE root afterward. Pooled holdout metrics weight each eligible contest equally; the 2017 and 2023 trained folds are also reported separately. Signed overall bias cancels because each complete predicted and observed slate sums to one; verify this accounting identity, and report signed bias by party/category, no-group status and slate-size stratum (2–5, 6–8, 9+ candidates). Category diagnostics use election-local source labels only; unverified person entrants are **unknown**, not guessed. Winner ranking is the exact argmax set with a `1e-12` absolute share tie tolerance. Report uniquely predicted winner accuracy over all eligible contests, predicted tie count and tied-set actual-winner inclusion separately. Uniform must produce ties. No winner probability or proper probability score is produced.

The materiality screen is an aggregate contest-equal MAE gain of at least **0.25pp** against uniform, with positive gains separately in 2017 and 2023, no RMSE regression of **0.25pp or more** in either trained fold, and no loss of full-frame or no-group coverage. Show 2011 separately. This diagnostic screen is not a significance test and cannot select an operational 2026 model; that selection stays null. Three dependent development election clusters cannot provide untouched confirmation.

## Party-input substitutions and abstentions

Fit κ only with earlier **observed** local party vectors. Freeze each fold's κ for Stage 5 additive, proportional and log-odds substitutions. A point substitution is allowed only when one target contest has an exact party-key match to *every* registered group, each Stage 5 prediction is a finite degenerate point interval, every point is nonnegative and the **joint vector sums to one within 1e-9** without clipping or renormalization. Unmatched groups, different party coverage, nondegenerate/coupled bounds or a failed total cause an explicit point abstention. Do not stitch marginal predictions or choose interval midpoints. Stage 5 substitutions remain conditional on observed target national support and turnout, not as-of inputs. If no fully coherent point exists, report that branch unsupported; a justified joint set may be considered only in a later separately specified stage. Stage 5's transform choice remains unresolved.

Construction and evaluation are separate saved artifacts. Construction reads official candidate IDs/affiliations, observed target party votes and earlier training outcomes only; target candidate votes, winner flags, person links and residuals are excluded from prediction inputs. Evaluation reads target candidate totals after construction is committed. Save exact input hashes, source snapshot, code hashes, fitted κ/training coverage, full-frame abstentions, construction shares, evaluation actuals and metrics. Reproduction must reject changed/deleted/ambiguous consumed source records, altered raw bytes or changed processed inputs, while allowing unrelated source additions.

Adding common κ cannot alter within-slate candidate ranking; all no-group candidates tie, and none can outrank a candidate with positive mapped party support. It does not model candidate-specific strength. Party support without a candidate is redistributed through the denominator as an assumption. No historical effect bonus is stacked. Performance on unchanged-boundary contests cannot validate candidate transport to changed 2026 boundaries. Operational selection stays null.
