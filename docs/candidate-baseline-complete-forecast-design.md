## 2. Minimum forecast and information contract

The primary outcome is a vector of shares of **valid candidate votes**, one nonnegative entry for **every standing candidate** (registered party, entrant or independent), summing to one in each held contest. A candidate-vote **count** additionally needs a forecast of valid candidate ballots. That denominator is distinct from valid party ballots used to define party support. If counts or a joint ballot ledger are requested, forecast votes cast, informal and disallowed candidate ballots consistently; candidate-only and party-only voting need not be equated. A cancelled/no-valid-candidate contest is an explicit abstention. Winner uncertainty requires a joint distribution or coupled scenario set over the full slate; marginal candidate intervals cannot be independently clipped or normalized. Shared national/party input errors must move related seats together.

Three tasks must remain labelled separately:

1. **Conditional historical diagnostic:** observed target local party shares and retrospective official slate are supplied. It diagnoses candidate allocation only; target candidate votes/winner flags remain outcomes.
2. **Supplied scenario:** a complete target-boundary party-support vector, slate and, for counts, candidate-turnout/validity controls are supplied by the user or another model. The result is conditional on those assumptions; a national-support scenario is **not** a national-support forecast.
3. **As-of historical or live 2026 forecast:** all party support, nominations, candidate-valid turnout and validity inputs must be traceable to a fixed pre-result cutoff. The proposed historical cutoff is the official nomination-close instant for each election, with a source publication/availability record. Those dated bundles are not preserved now. A live 2026 forecast additionally needs the certified slate, target-boundary party forecast and correlated uncertainty. Neither task can be claimed from conditional results or Stage 5 sensitivities using observed target national support/turnout.

Māori seats stay visible in every coverage table. Their seven-seat election clusters are too small for an independently fitted route under the proposed minimum evidence gate; do not silently borrow general-seat parameters. Report an explicit Māori abstention pending a justified common model and validation.

## 3. Three designs considered

| Design | Exact baseline and party input | Coverage and failure | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Geographic candidate-count transport | Try to allocate source candidate votes onto target boundaries, then match them to target people. Needs voter-residence×candidate joint counts, special-vote geography, verified same-person/candidacy applicability and candidate-valid denominator. | The preserved voting-place table does not supply voter residence; changed seats and replacements are unidentified. Only 117 historical leads lie on certified unchanged geography, nine source leads are cancelled, and no 2026 slate exists. Broad abstention and untestable personal transfer. | **Do not use as default.** Retain exact unchanged-seat historical counts for later restricted comparators only. |
| Joint party/candidate ballot transport | Carry a source joint split route to every target candidate and noncandidate destination, with target party/turnout controls. | Stage 15's primary set is wholly free in 157/191 contests and all 34 partly constrained primary contests reject the observed joint candidate vector. Its source-seat hull is not calibrated future uncertainty. Candidate turnover and unmatched destinations remain decisive. | Retain as conditional diagnostic, not a complete point or operational baseline. |
| **Direct party-anchored candidate-share composition** | Predict the full valid-candidate share vector from a supplied target-boundary party-share scenario and the target slate, with one strictly positive, training-only neutral support floor. No person or predecessor candidate count is required. | Can represent party entrants and independents, including on changed boundaries, but allocation of support from parties without a candidate and floor strength are assumptions. Needs as-of inputs and held-out calibration for a forecast. | **Recommended next testable baseline**, conditional first. |

The direct model answers a different, narrower question from Stage 15: how to allocate valid **candidate** shares conditional on a complete local party vector. It does not claim to reconstruct which party-vote group supplied each candidate ballot. It avoids an invented target-boundary candidate count and an outgoing person's premium.

## 4. Recommended baseline equation and gates

For held seat `e`, let `C_e` be the complete target slate. Supply a target-boundary vector `p_e,g ≥ 0` over all registered party-vote groups, `Σ_g p_e,g = 1`; a category reported only inside an unresolved “other” group is **missing**, not zero. At most one target candidate maps uniquely to any party `g`. Classify an independent or an unregistered affiliation with verified **no party-ballot group** explicitly as `partyKey=null, noRegisteredPartyGroup=true`; a null party key without that affirmative classification is ambiguous and causes abstention. Set `b_c=p_e,g(c)` for a mapped party candidate and `b_c=0` for an explicitly no-group candidate. For a strictly positive common `κ`, define

`w_e,c = b_c + κ`, and `m_e,c = w_e,c / Σ_{d∈C_e} w_e,d`.

`m_e` is a nonnegative, complete valid-candidate-share composition. An independent, verified unregistered-affiliation candidate or a party candidate with explicit zero scenario support receives **possible positive mass** via `κ`. Parties with votes but no standing candidate do not become invisible: their support is redistributed among the standing candidates through the common denominator. That is a **neutral modelling assumption**, not a claim about actual split-ticket ballots. The floor is not an estimated personal effect; the absence of a person adjustment does not assert zero unknown candidate strength. Replacements and continuations obey the same identity-free baseline, and their uncertainty must remain broad until independently validated. The synthetic kernel in `scripts/checkpoints/candidate_share_design.py` demonstrates the equation only.

For a later authorized historical implementation, fit the **single** `κ` using completed earlier **general** elections only, without tuning it to a target holdout: constrain `κ∈[0.0001,0.1]` in party-share units and minimize equal-contest cross entropy `|T|⁻¹ Σ_{e∈T} −Σ_{c∈C_e} s_e,c log m_e,c(κ)`, where `s` uses observed earlier valid-candidate votes. Fix deterministic one-dimensional optimization, numerical tolerance and any boundary-optimum report before implementation. Require at least 20 complete earlier held general contests and five with an independent or explicitly zero-support standing candidate; otherwise abstain on fitted allocation. This floor range is a preregistered **proposal**, not a fitted result, and a boundary solution does not authorize expanding the range after seeing holdout errors. Pool over all eligible earlier general contests rather than selecting identities or winners. Do not fit a Māori-specific `κ` from seven-seat clusters.

If a complete party vector, uniquely mapped slate or valid-candidate denominator (for counts) is missing, abstain on the corresponding output. A supplied party vector may be an exact scenario or a **joint** feasible draw from Stage 4's coupled notional party bounds and a named Stage 5 transform scenario. Never independently combine marginal bound endpoints or call the synthetic 2023 baseline a 2026 party forecast. With only bounds and no justified point scenario, output a coupled scenario set or abstain on a point. If future counts are requested, multiply each `m_e,c` by **forecast valid candidate votes**, with `votes cast = valid candidate + informal candidate + disallowed candidate` ballots. Party-valid votes remain a different denominator. No winner probability is produced without a calibrated joint scenario distribution.

Uncertainty belongs to the **whole slate and shared inputs**. Future forecast draws, if validated, must take one coherent party-support scenario across related seats, one common fitted-floor uncertainty draw, and a joint candidate residual draw, then recompute the entire simplex. Independent per-candidate clipping and renormalization are prohibited. With the present evidence, report the scenario winner set or abstain on probabilistic winner output; do not label uncalibrated spreads probabilities. Unknown entrants/independents need explicit residual coverage diagnostics and may require wider uncertainty or abstention. Stage 7 residuals, Stage 8 persistence, Stage 9 freshman, Stage 10 replacement, Stage 16 source victory and Stage 11/15 split patterns are **not added as bonuses**. Any future complete view must replace, not stack on, the same candidate-share baseline.

## 5. Frozen proposed validation protocol, before any Stage 17 scoring

The design has already been informed by 2011/2017/2023 Stage 5–16 findings. These are **development** holdouts, not untouched confirmatory elections. Chronological reuse cannot create new independent election clusters. A later genuinely untouched election or independently archived forecast bundle is needed for a stronger operational claim.

- **Full frame:** the existing 213 comparable source/target contests, including 191 held general candidate contests, 21 Māori coverage-only contests and cancelled Port Waikato. Evaluate a direct-share conditional diagnostic on all 191 held general targets, with every one of their 1,313 candidate occurrences, including losers, independents and entrants. No outcome/identity-based trimming. Keep Māori and cancellation abstentions explicit. The 2023→2026 71-seat inventory is a **target-boundary applicability** frame, never a 2026 scoring set.
- **Chronology:** for target 2011 train `κ` only on completed 2008 held general elections; for 2017 on 2008–2014, and for 2023 on 2008–2020. No target/later candidate totals, winner flags, residuals or split cells may set `κ`, eligibility, party mapping or fallback. Historical candidate slates and target observed party shares enter **only** the labelled conditional diagnostic. A strict as-of branch abstains unless the official nomination-close cutoff and dated party/slate/turnout bundle are preserved.
- **Common samples and benchmarks:** full-frame coverage/abstention first. Compare the one-parameter composition with a parameter-free `1/|C_e|` uniform-share baseline on identical held-general contests. A no-floor party-proportional rule is a **restricted** comparator only for contests with no independent/unsupported candidate and positive mapped support; report its full-frame abstention and compare on its identical subset. Do not call a model better by dropping hard cases. The Stage 11 deferred carry-forward and source candidate-share comparators require verified geography and identity, and remain separately deferred.
- **Errors:** report contest-equal mean of within-contest candidate-share MAE, RMSE and signed bias in percentage points, plus candidate-equal sensitivity and winner top-share hit rate where a point exists. Report strata by holdout, party, entrant/independent presence, slate size, changed/unchanged boundary and Māori coverage. For counts, score only on separately identified identical samples with pre-result valid-candidate turnout inputs. For probabilistic winners, require joint draws and report proper Brier/log scores plus reliability and interval coverage; if no calibrated draws, report **null**, not invented confidence. Out-of-fold errors across all candidates and seats must retain shared party/election dependence; no candidate-iid significance claims.
- **Materiality and stopping:** use the existing 0.25 percentage-point aggregate MAE threshold as an indicative screen, with positive gain in both trained 2017 and 2023 folds and no material RMSE or entrant/independent coverage regression. Show 2011 separately and require all four coverage groups above. This is not a significance threshold and **cannot** select an operational 2026 forecast from conditional observed-party tests. Stop at a documented null/unresolved status if as-of inputs, broad coverage or joint uncertainty validation are absent. Do not retune floor bounds, categories, frame, benchmarks or Stage 5 transforms after inspecting errors. Compare additive/proportional/log-odds party scenarios separately, retaining Stage 5's unresolved choice.

## 6. Exact next bounded implementation requiring separate authorization

Implement **only** the identity-free conditional direct-share baseline on the frozen 213-contest frame, scoring the 191 held general contests against the uniform benchmark and reporting the restricted party-only comparator on its common subset. Fit the single `κ` chronologically from earlier observed elections under the equation above. First build an auditable within-election party/category classifier from preserved official candidature and party groups; classify verified no-party-group candidates affirmatively and abstain on ambiguous affiliations rather than assigning them zero support. Produce separate construction inputs and evaluation-only actuals/scores, deterministic source/processed-input manifests, full-frame coverage/abstention and target-outcome mutation tests. Reproduce prior outputs exactly. An affected seat abstains on missing mapping or denominator; a global source-count, chronology or conservation failure stops calculation until corrected. Do not relax the rule to improve coverage. No new acquisition is needed for this **conditional** implementation, so its resource cap is **zero**. It would test a complete candidate-share allocation *given observed local party support*, not a live or genuinely as-of forecast.

After that separate review, a strict as-of forecast would still require another authorization to obtain dated historical nomination-close slates and party/turnout inputs, a 2026 target slate and party forecast/scenario, and defensible joint uncertainty. The crosswalk's 2023 synthetic party bounds can inform scenario inputs on 2025 geography; they cannot create missing candidate votes or current support. Stage 6 candidate-baseline response remains blocked until a separately validated candidate-specific target-boundary reference is available. No ensemble weighting, 2026 polling/Opportunity modelling or forecast production follows automatically.
