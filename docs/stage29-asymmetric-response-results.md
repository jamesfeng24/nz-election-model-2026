## Interpretation and next decision

The formal gate-first test provides no eligible temporal comparison. The supplement reveals numerically estimable in-sample associations with generally larger toward slopes, but no amplified-above-one toward response, National anchor-label disagreement, and Labour response sensitivity to2020→2023. Forecasting improvement cannot be determined from these descriptive fits. Do not call them a stable predictive pattern or deploy their coefficients. Selected exact seats, overlapping transitions, turnover/leadership/election conditions and only five reused transition environments limit inference. Historical publication by an as-of cutoff remains unknown; observed target national/local support are conditional oracle inputs.

S remains the preferred complete-share development candidate, with baseline mandatory. This partial-party response diagnostic cannot be added as a bonus to S. All operational selections, earlier outputs and identity adjudications remain unchanged. Recommend stopping this asymmetric formulation under its frozen gates and separately authorizing expanded normalized-residual persistence using Stage26 broad primary and strict sensitivity, without reopening its exception queue. No automatic older-election acquisition or alternative anchor.
