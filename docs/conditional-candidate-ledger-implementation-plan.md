## Exact construction and optimization

For each source-election party origin, include every complete, uniquely mapped held general-seat split row with exact positive `totalPartyVotes`. Validate its full candidate/noncandidate cell set, source candidate IDs and category continuity. Apply closed two-decimal percent bounds to **cells** and require each row's sum to equal one. Aggregate candidate cells by canonical party category only within this coupled row. A complete source seat lacking a candidate of category `h` contributes structural zero to `h` while retaining its origin-row weight. A printed zero retains its rounding interval. Missing/inconsistent rows are excluded with reason and mass, never imputed; absent/zero-mass origin rows have no weight. Cross-election party joins use only the frozen eligible continuity records; ambiguous or report-only joins do not become synonyms.

The primary feasible route vector is the exact fixed-row-mass weighted image of these source cell simplexes. The separate heterogeneity set is their convex hull. For any **linear** contest objective, compute sharp extrema by a deterministic capped-simplex support function on each source row: start at every cell lower bound, allocate remaining row probability to cells in objective order within their upper capacities. The primary support is the exact count-weighted sum of row supports; the heterogeneity support is the best/worst eligible source-row support, reflecting its convex hull. Whole-origin free routing uses the best/worst standing-candidate or noncandidate destination. An absent target destination makes only its coupled source-category mass free; a new/independent/ambiguous target candidate makes the entire origin free, superseding absent-category release. Personal identity labels never enter routing.

Candidate-vote and valid-candidate-denominator bounds are linear support queries on this **same** contest ledger. For share bounds when the sharp denominator lower bound is positive, solve `max/min(candidateVotes - r × validCandidateVotes)=0` by deterministic bisection on `r∈[0,1]`, using the same support function; stop at ratio width ≤ `1e-9` and verify endpoint objective residual ≤ `1e-5` ballot. A denominator lower bound at or below `1e-8` ballot returns `undefined` share and point abstention. Ballot-mass and support feasibility residual tolerances are `1e-8` in fractions and `1e-5` ballots; failures are numerical errors, never zero-filled or widened substantive bounds. A point value is emitted only when its exact input-constrained vote bounds coincide within `1e-8` ballot, not from an interval midpoint.

Joint observed-candidate-vector feasibility is **evaluation only**. Use SciPy HiGHS `linprog` (the exact pinned `scipy==1.16.0` already in `requirements-boundaries.txt`) for a single contest-wide linear feasibility system with one common set of source-row cell variables, fixed weights, explicit absent-category free routing and target candidate equations. The heterogeneity case adds convex-combination weights with scaled row-cell bounds. Check equality, inequality and variable-bound residuals independently to `1e-5` ballot (`1e-8` probability). Distinguish feasible, mathematically infeasible, unbounded/invalid formulation and numerical/solver failure. An unrelated candidate-result discrepancy cannot modify construction. Cache reusable source-origin geometry but do not learn from target outcomes.

## Evaluation and stopping

Generate all four frozen methods on the same 191-contest conditional frame: primary pool, separate source-seat convex-hull sensitivity, party diagonal and unrestricted ledger. For every held general target candidate, report finite/informative versus unrestricted vote bounds, share/denominator status, point abstention, interval width, actual inclusion and joint-vector feasibility. Compare on declared common candidate/contest sets, with holdout, party, source support and applicability counts. Use exact published candidate totals and candidate-ballot valid denominator **only** after construction is written and committed. Compute point MAE/RMSE/bias only where both methods genuinely have points. Broad intervals containing outcomes are not evidence of calibrated predictive value. Record how many contests become wholly free due to unsupported or independent candidates.

If any preserved population control is inconsistent, source rows cannot be jointly reconciled, solver residuals exceed tolerance, or the common evaluated sample cannot be formed, preserve an explicit failure/abstention and investigate before scoring. Do not tune routing, add pseudocounts, change the cohort or widen intervals after seeing results. This is conditional on observed target party/turnout inputs and retrospective nominations, not a complete pre-election forecast. Operational selection remains null; Stage 6, 2026 boundary, identity acquisition, integration and ensemble work remain excluded.
