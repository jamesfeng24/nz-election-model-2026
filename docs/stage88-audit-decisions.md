## D128 (J2): the configuration pins the electorate-poll run

- `seatPolls.electorateRun = {date, pollsSha256}` in `config/nowcast-2026.json` names one run of `data/processed/polling/electorate-live/`. The validator requires it whenever seat polls are enabled, and checks it against the index.
- The assembly, its seat evidence and the Stage79 readout read that run (`scripts/polling/electorate_live.py`), not the newest. A hash mismatch fails the build. The bank's `inputs` record `electorateRun` and `electorateRunSha256`.
- Māori seat polls are cut at `national.dataCutoff`, as the general-seat polls already were. The seat evidence lists a later poll as `not-used`, "fieldwork ended after the data cutoff".
- `python3 -m scripts.polling.weekly_refresh.adopt --date D [--electorate-date E]` sets the run together with the national input (default: the newest run).
- **Effect:** none on today's numbers. Pinned run 2026-10-10 (12 polls): every poll ended by the 2026-10-07 cutoff, and the Stage79 readout reproduces unchanged.
- The development gate, the synthetic bank fixture and the Stage79 readout now run in the CI `live` job, because merging a poll refresh no longer changes them.

## D129 (J3): a party-list party's electorate win inside Other is overhang

- The bank carries `partyVote.ballotPartyIds`: the 17 registered parties with a 2026 party list (`party-lists.json` beside the roster's candidate features). Every simulated party group must be among them.
- The seat layer takes the ones simulated inside the Other bucket as `zeroVotePartyIds` (`src/models/mmp/seatLayer.ts`). Their electorate winner qualifies the party with zero party votes of its own (s 191(4)(b)), earns no Sainte-Laguë seat and is overhang (s 192(5)). Parliament grows to 121 in such a draw instead of the other parties sharing 119.
- Winners with no party (independents) stay s 191(8), inside the 120.
- In 2026 this is chiefly Te Tai Tokerau Party in Te Tai Tokerau, which the Stage78 fallback gives about 13% of draws.
- **Effect, development live bank (1,024 national draws × 4, seat layer run with and without the rule):** a bucketed list party wins an electorate in 13.1% of draws (Te Tai Tokerau Party, Stage78 fallback). Mean Parliament size rises from 124.73 to 124.83. The extra seat partly offsets, because the other parties now share 120 entitlements instead of 119, which absorbs some existing overhang. Each listed party's mean seat count moves by at most 0.04. Bloc majority probabilities move by at most 0.002 (NAT+ACT+NZF 0.278 to 0.276; LAB+GRN+TPM 0.050 to 0.049). The rule also covers the other bucketed list parties (Conservative, Loyal and the rest), which win no electorate in these draws.
- **Approximation:** the party's real vote stays in Other. A Te Tai Tokerau Party vote large enough for one list seat (about 0.4%) would make the seat a list-entitled seat, not overhang; this is not modelled.

## D127 (J1): the Māori C–P range

- Every polled Māori seat is also simulated under Stage71's arm P:
  - the Stage71 control fit on all four elections (identical to Stage66's);
  - σ² and τ² multiplied by a λ drawn per draw from Stage71's 1,000-replicate bootstrap, rebuilt exactly (`scripts/maori_seat_calibration/inflation.py`; the stored 5/50/95% λ quantiles reproduce);
  - Stage71's readout simulator on its own seed streams.
- The record keeps P's winners only (`inflationWinners`).
- The snapshot gives each candidate `winProbabilityInflation {p, mcse, ess}` beside `winProbability` (C). The release gate holds both to the MCSE ≤ 0.01 threshold.
- The seat totals, MMP and `electoratePredictions` stay on C, one law per draw. The site was to show C and P as the labelled range; D131 (2026-10-10) superseded that on display, and the site shows C only. The export, the gate and this build are unchanged.
- Seats on the Stage78 fallback (three today) have no P: Stage78 froze no inflation of the fallback. They keep their single labelled fallback number.
- **Development run (65,536 draws), leader's win probability, C to P:**

| Seat | C | P |
|---|---|---|
| Hauraki-Waikato | 0.88 | 0.74 |
| Te Tai Hauāuru | 0.74 | 0.64 |
| Te Tai Tonga | 0.84 | 0.61 |
| Waiariki | 0.97 | 0.82 |

These match Stage71's 2026 readout pattern. P lowers every leader; its λ is about 3, with a 90% interval of 1.7 to 7.0.
