# Frozen next-test design: candidate balance dispersion

**Design only. No challenger is fitted in Stage47.** Implementation requires separate authorization. This post-result design uses repeatedly inspected development elections; chronological parameter fitting will not turn it into untouched validation.

The machine contract is [next-test-contract.json](../data/processed/uncertainty-expectation/next-test-contract.json). It freezes exactly three Gaussian restrictions:

1. Numerically corrected Stage45 control.
2. Independently fitted, earlier-trained constant candidate-seat N/L balance scale adjustment.
3. Independently fitted, strongly pooled conditional adjustment in that same direction.

Local, shared-candidate, major-mass, remainder, national, zero-face, mean-coefficient and transport laws are unchanged. No Student-t, new covariance, mean reset, prior-strength search or added transport variance.

## Equation and information

Seat scale is `sigma_i = sigma_frozen,e × exp(a + b_R(x_R−m_R) + b_T(x_T−m_T))`. The constant restriction refits a with b=0; the control fixes all to0. Shared election scale remains frozen. Supported R deficit is `1−mean supportedMass.R` for the unique National/Labour pair. Historical nonmajor support is the continuous complete-source candidate-share proxy under the frozen incoming-population point, not future strength or reconstructed target votes.

The structural characteristic replaces fragmentation. Source support shows some within-election balance-dispersion association, while fragmentation does not consistently identify greater error. R support also has mixed evidence; strong shrinkage and an independently refitted constant are necessary. Neither association is treated as a known mechanism. No contender-departure or new-strength variable is inferred from a nonmatch, an eventual result or an after-cutoff announcement.

Centers use equal-election/equal-seat permitted training records only. Missing inputs contribute neutral centered values with flags. Missing major candidate directions retain the unchanged applicable mass/remainder law. No useful slate is removed merely for missing features.

## Fitting and pooling

The Gaussian log-ratio likelihood integrates the existing shared election effect, using `Sigma_e = diag(sigma_i²)+sigma_shared,e² 11ᵀ`. Conditional logistic locations preserve the frozen arithmetic ratio means under those total variances. This is numerical mean preservation, not a fitted mean correction. The unchanged1e−6 observation replacement retains standing-zero evidence; prediction zeros remain locked.

Minimize the equal-environment likelihood normalized by each environment's seat count plus fixed ridge penalties with log-scale SD0.5 on a and both b terms, centered at0. One prior SD is a factor1.65 over an entire unit of a proportion. No holdout tuning. Fixed log-multiplier coefficient bounds ±log4, fixed starts and independent optimum/gradient checks are explicit in the machine contract; bound contacts are reported, never expanded after results. Likelihood rank and penalty-defined estimability are reported separately.

Earlier residual environments:2017 uses2014;2020 uses2014/17;2023 uses2014/17/20.2014 has none and retains a=b=0, explicitly prior/control. Saved historical candidate means retain their own earlier fitted coefficients. All257 complete component seats and the existing193 composed56-day cases remain; no added forecasts or political data.
