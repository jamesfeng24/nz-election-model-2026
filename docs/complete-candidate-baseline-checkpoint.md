# Complete historical candidate-vote baseline: executable design checkpoint

**Status (2026-09-26): proposed specification for independent review.** PR #20 merged as `412cd7b`, containing reviewed Stage 13 head `a9489dc`. This checkpoint reads preserved data only and fits no parameter, generates no candidate prediction or historical score, and makes no operational selection. The adopted constraints below retain the Stage 8–10 and Stage 11 null operational selections; null is not a real-world zero effect. Stage 5/6 choices remain unresolved. The machine-readable [specification](../data/processed/checkpoints/complete-candidate-baseline/specification.json), [contracts](../data/processed/checkpoints/complete-candidate-baseline/contracts.json), [input inventory](../data/processed/checkpoints/complete-candidate-baseline/input-inventory.json) and [manifest](../data/processed/checkpoints/complete-candidate-baseline/manifest.json) are the reviewable checkpoint.

## Task and evidence boundary

The **conditional historical diagnostic** would predict the valid candidate-vote allocation in a held target contest given its *observed* local party-vote groups, and, if explicitly declared, observed informal-party and turnout controls. Target candidature currently comes from post-election official tables; a historical nomination-close publication audit has not been completed. Target candidate totals, winner flags and split **destination percentages** are evaluation-only. This diagnostic cannot be described as a complete pre-election forecast.

A **genuine pre-election forecast** needs a source-pinned official nomination-close cutoff for each target year, and nominations, national/local party support, votes cast, informal/disallowed ballot rates and routing evidence demonstrably available by that cutoff. The exact historic cutoff dates and publication timing are not pinned. None of these forecast input bundles is complete in the preserved inventory. An externally supplied **national-support scenario** is a conditional input, not a national forecast; it still needs separate turnout, ballot-validity, nomination and local-allocation inputs. Stage 5's historical target national shares and Stage 11's target turnout sensitivity are observed diagnostics, not as-of forecasts.

The full validation frame is all **213** independently validated unchanged-boundary seat transitions: 70 in 2008→2011, 71 in 2014→2017 and 72 in 2020→2023. This includes 192 general and 21 Māori contests. One general 2023 Port Waikato contest is cancelled/unheld; the other **212** are held, containing **1,392 target candidature occurrences** (1,313 general, 79 Māori). The Stage 13 30-seat/415-occurrence identity sample does not select this frame. Source and target seat numbers plus the validated regime establish geography; matching seat names alone do not. All target winners, losers, entrants, independents and unknown identity cases remain in coverage reporting. The proposed earliest holdout (2011) can use 2008 source split rows, but any parameter requiring an earlier target transition must abstain.

The [inventory](../data/processed/checkpoints/complete-candidate-baseline/input-inventory.json) has one row per contest and deliberately contains no outcome values. For 191 held general contests, source local matrices and independently assembled *observed* target party/turnout/candidate-result records exist. The 192nd general source matrix belongs to cancelled Port Waikato. One 2020 Māori source matrix is preserved as supporting evidence, but it does not make a comparable Māori panel; the 21 Māori targets lack the same assembled complete local split/party-group path. Raw official Māori party/candidate material remains preserved, not zero-filled. Strict as-of readiness is **0/213** because cutoff dates and forecast inputs are unverified or missing. These availability counts are not prediction coverage or scores.

## Recommended identity-free baseline and comparators

**Proposal for review:** construct a *pooled party-destination interval ledger* using only source-election general-seat split rows earlier than each target. Each row has an exact party-ballot group size and two-decimal **rounded** conditional destination percentages; exact joint counts are unavailable. Group source candidate destinations by their **party category**, not person or source-seat candidate. For a source-year party-ballot group, pool row counts and reported rounding intervals across eligible source seats under the Stage 5 party-continuity contract. The pooled lower/upper numerator is the sum of each exact row size times its permitted percentage endpoint, divided by total eligible source row mass; do not use arbitrary cell midpoints as observations. Preserve a source-seat heterogeneity envelope and the joint row-sum constraint. If a party/category has no eligible earlier rows, it is an explicit unknown route, not an estimated zero. A later implementation must state its pooling sample and demonstrate feasible joint row probabilities before scoring.

The target ledger begins with all cast ballots. Partition them into each valid party-vote group, an informal-party group, and the remainder whose party ballot is disallowed or unrepresented in the published split rows. In a conditional exercise, independently published target party group totals and turnout controls provide these masses. In a forecast, they must be forecast or bounded as of cutoff. Every group routes jointly to **every standing candidate** and the noncandidate destinations (informal candidate ballot and no valid candidate vote, including party-vote-only/candidate-disallowed cases). All route fractions are nonnegative and sum to one for that group. Valid candidate votes are the sum of named-candidate destinations, including any candidate-only ballots originating in the informal/disallowed-party groups. Published candidate share divides by *valid candidate votes*, not valid party votes or split-row total. If that denominator can be zero, share is undefined and a point prediction abstains.

Map a pooled destination *party category* to a target candidate only if one standing candidate of a continuing party unambiguously represents it. This does not assert that a replacement inherited an outgoing individual's premium. A source destination whose party has no target candidate, an entrant party-ballot group, an independent, ambiguous alliance/party switch, missing destination or uncertain identity keeps its mass in a **joint feasible unknown route** across candidate and noncandidate destinations. Do not clip candidates separately or renormalize the surviving destinations. Candidate vote and denominator bounds are marginal projections of one contest-wide feasible ledger; their upper endpoints need not be jointly attainable. Candidate-share extrema must be optimized over the same joint feasible ledger, not formed by dividing unrelated marginal endpoints. Any Stage 5 transformed party vector whose groups cannot sum with informal/disallowed mass to a common votes-cast total is rejected or widened; it is not secretly renormalized. A route with unresolved mass may yield an interval but no justified point estimate. Unknown candidate history remains uncertainty rather than zero strength.

Two deliberately simple comparators suffice: (1) a **party-diagonal accounting** rule, assigning a valid party group to its unique same-party candidate as a stated simplification while preserving all other/unmatched mass as unknown; and (2) an **unrestricted feasible ledger**, which tests whether historical pooled routing narrows bounds without losing coverage. Point MAE/RMSE against the diagonal is meaningful only on an identical set where both methods have a predeclared point rule. Stage 11's local source-seat transfer is a separate later sensitivity: transporting a local named-candidate destination to a replacement may transport outgoing strength, so it is **not** the identity-free default. Its specified but unimplemented probable-continuation carry-forward sensitivity also remains deferred and requires justified identity/geography. Stage 7 residuals and Stage 8–10 descriptive terms are never stacked onto this ledger.

## Frozen validation proposal, before any scoring

For each of the 2011, 2017 and 2023 held-out targets, source patterns and any pooled or fallback quantities must use elections **strictly earlier** than the target. Target party votes may enter only the labelled conditional task; target split destination cells, candidate votes and winner flags are evaluation-only. Freeze the full 212-held-contest frame first. Report eligible candidates and contest counts, point/interval coverage and abstentions on that full frame; then score models and comparators on a separately identified identical candidate intersection. Do not discard difficult entrants, replacements, unknown identity or losing candidates to improve error. Report general and Māori separately, transition/party and meaningful status strata where evidence exists. Three election transitions, repeated seats and shared party references limit uncertainty claims.

If point estimates are separately justified later, calculate equal-candidate-weight MAE, RMSE and signed bias in valid candidate votes and in percentage points of **valid candidate votes**, with n and common-sample IDs. Contest-weighted results are a declared sensitivity. For joint intervals, report exact observed-total inclusion, mean/median width, denominator-undefined count, full-frame interval coverage and abstention, plus contest-level conservation failures. Candidate marginal bounds are not a joint prediction of all upper endpoints. Never score a conditional result as an as-of forecast. A candidate/party routing parameter must be frozen before scoring and estimated only from allowed earlier elections. Do not tune smoothing or fallback to recover non-null operational selection.

No operational point baseline can be selected from this checkpoint. Later selection would require verified as-of inputs, coherent routing and denominator, nontrivial prespecified frame coverage, and stable chronological improvement over the party-diagonal rule on identical samples without sacrificing interval calibration or conservation. The unrestricted ledger checks the value and sharpness of historical information. Stop if timing is unknown for required forecast inputs, any ballot group lacks a coherent bounded route, the denominator can be zero without an undefined state, or no common sample exists. Retain null/unresolved selection and bounds rather than inventing exact votes.

## Reproduction and preservation

`python3 -m scripts.checkpoints.complete_candidate_baseline --check` regenerates the 213-row availability inventory from the Stage 12 geography/occurrence frame and preserved election/split files, verifying pinned hashes. `python3 -m unittest scripts.tests.test_complete_candidate_baseline_checkpoint -v` runs synthetic conservation, absent-destination, denominator, missingness and target-outcome-invariance checks. The synthetic ballots live only in tests. No historical candidate prediction or score is generated. Stage 5–11 numerical outputs, Stage 13 evidence, raw files and operational selections are unchanged.
